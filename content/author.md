## Who am I?

I'm **Phil Albert-Benoist**, founder, editor and director of publication of *MyDogCanFly*. My background combines digital engineering (EFREI Paris) and visual culture (ENSP Arles). I oversee the platform architecture, editorial standards, sourcing methodology, multilingual content and publication of the site.

## My approach

I collect and structure information from primary official sources. Verified claims include the source, the quoted passage and the verification date. Information that has not yet met that standard is identified as still to be confirmed rather than presented as established. MyDogCanFly does not provide veterinary advice.
