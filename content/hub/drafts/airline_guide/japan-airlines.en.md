# Flying with a Dog on Japan Airlines (JAL)

## Overview

Japan Airlines (JAL) is Japan's flag carrier and a member of the **oneworld** alliance. From its hubs at **Tokyo Haneda (HND), Tokyo Narita (NRT), Osaka Kansai (KIX)** and **Osaka Itami (ITM)**, JAL serves destinations throughout Japan, Asia, Europe, North America and Oceania.

For companion animals, JAL follows a policy similar to ANA. **Companion dogs travel in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold** and are **not accepted in the passenger cabin**, except for recognised assistance dogs.

Travelling to Japan also requires compliance with one of the world's strictest import procedures, including microchipping, rabies vaccinations, antibody testing, advance notification and approval from the Japanese Animal Quarantine Service.

MyDogCanFly analyses both the airline's transport policy and Japan's import regulations before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Japan Airlines a good choice?

Yes. Japan Airlines is internationally recognised for its operational reliability and its experience transporting companion animals safely. Its main advantages include an extensive domestic Japanese network, worldwide international routes, specialised live animal handling, excellent punctuality, and a modern fleet. As with any trip to Japan, the airline is only one part of the process—the import regulations are equally important.

# Cabin, hold or assistance dogs

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_japan_airlines | cabin_policy}}

Companion dogs are **not accepted in the passenger cabin**. Only recognised assistance dogs travelling with eligible passengers may remain in the cabin.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_japan_airlines | hold_policy}}

Companion dogs travel inside the aircraft's ventilated and temperature-controlled hold. Requirements include advance reservation; an IATA-compliant rigid kennel; valid veterinary documentation; and acceptance depending on aircraft type and weather conditions. Certain extreme temperatures may temporarily suspend pet transport.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_japan_airlines | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds may be restricted because of increased respiratory risks. Additional seasonal restrictions may also apply.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify Japan's import regulations;
- ensure your dog has an ISO-compatible microchip;
- complete all rabies vaccination requirements;
- perform the rabies antibody blood test if required;
- notify Japan's Animal Quarantine Service within the required deadline;
- reserve your dog's transport with Japan Airlines.

Failure to comply with Japan's import rules may result in quarantine or refusal of entry even if your flight reservation has been confirmed.

# Common mistakes

## Beginning Japan's import process too late

Preparation may require several months.

## Assuming airline approval guarantees entry

Only the Japanese authorities decide whether an animal may enter the country.

## Using a kennel that does not meet IATA standards

Only compliant kennels are accepted.

## Ignoring seasonal restrictions

Weather conditions may affect the transport of certain breeds.

# Expert advice

Japan Airlines is one of the best airlines for travelling with a dog to Japan. The most important part of the journey is preparing Japan's import documentation. Begin planning several months before departure and ensure every veterinary requirement has been completed before booking your flight.
