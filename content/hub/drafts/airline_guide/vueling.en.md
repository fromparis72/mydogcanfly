# Flying with a Dog on Vueling

## Overview

Vueling is Spain's largest low-cost airline and one of Europe's leading short-haul carriers. Operating primarily from Barcelona-El Prat, with additional bases across Spain, France and Italy, it serves more than one hundred destinations throughout Europe and North Africa.

For dog owners, Vueling occupies a unique position among European low-cost airlines because it allows small pets to travel in the passenger cabin on many routes. This makes it an attractive option for weekend breaks, city trips and Mediterranean holidays.

However, travelling with your dog on Vueling still requires careful planning. A successful journey depends on three independent factors:

- Vueling's pet transport policy;
- the veterinary and customs requirements of your destination country;
- operational considerations such as route restrictions, seasonal demand and cabin pet availability.

# Is Vueling a good choice?

For many European travellers, yes. Unlike several other low-cost airlines, Vueling has built a reputation as one of the easiest budget carriers for passengers travelling with small dogs. Its extensive network across Spain, France, Italy and Portugal makes it especially attractive for leisure travel.

Its main limitation is that it focuses almost exclusively on short-haul routes. If your destination requires a long-haul connection, another airline may offer a simpler overall itinerary.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- check your destination;
- confirm pet availability;
- verify your dog's travel weight;
- ensure your carrier complies with Vueling's requirements.

Low-cost flights can fill quickly, particularly during school holidays.

# Travelling through Barcelona

Barcelona is Vueling's largest hub. If your itinerary includes a connection, remember that every flight must independently accept pets. Whenever possible, choose itineraries with comfortable transfer times.

# Common mistakes

## Assuming every low-cost airline accepts pets

Many do not. Vueling is one of the exceptions, making it particularly attractive for European travellers.

## Booking without reserving the pet

Pet capacity is limited. Always reserve your dog's place as early as possible.

## Ignoring destination regulations

Even within Europe, veterinary requirements continue to apply.

# Expert advice

If your dog is eligible for cabin travel, Vueling is often one of the simplest and most economical airlines in Europe. When comparing airlines, however, always compare complete itineraries rather than ticket prices alone.
