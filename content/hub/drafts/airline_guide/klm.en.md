# Flying with a Dog on KLM

## Overview

KLM Royal Dutch Airlines is the national airline of the Netherlands and a founding member of the Air France–KLM Group and the SkyTeam alliance. Operating primarily from **Amsterdam Airport Schiphol (AMS)**, KLM serves more than 160 destinations across Europe, North America, South America, Africa, Asia and the Caribbean.

For travellers flying with a dog, KLM is one of Europe's most pet-friendly network airlines. Depending on your dog's size, breed and destination, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Certain destinations or oversized animals may require cargo transport.

A successful journey always depends on three independent factors:

- KLM's transport policy;
- veterinary and customs regulations of the destination country;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

# Is KLM a good choice?

Absolutely. KLM has long been recognised as one of Europe's leading airlines for travelling with pets. Its main strengths include pets accepted in both cabin and hold, an excellent worldwide network, a highly efficient hub at Amsterdam Schiphol, harmonised procedures with Air France, and reliable long-haul operations. For many international journeys, KLM offers one of the smoothest experiences available.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_klm | cabin_policy}}

One cat or one dog may travel in the passenger cabin when flying in **Economy Class** or **Business Class within Europe**. The combined weight of the pet and carrier must not exceed **8 kg**, and the carrier must fit beneath the seat in front of you. Pets must remain inside the carrier throughout the flight. Cabin transport is **not available in Premium Comfort or intercontinental Business Class**, where under-seat space is insufficient.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_klm | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding the cabin limit may travel in the aircraft's ventilated and temperature-controlled hold inside an **IATA-compliant kennel**. The combined weight of the dog and kennel may not exceed **75 kg**. Certain aircraft have additional kennel size limitations, and pets are generally **not accepted in the hold when a connection exceeds three hours**. Some destinations also require transport via cargo.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_klm | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds and other breeds presenting increased transport risks may be subject to operational restrictions. Always verify eligibility before booking.

# Before booking

Before confirming your reservation:

- verify destination-country import requirements;
- ensure your dog is microchipped and properly vaccinated;
- select an IATA-compliant carrier or kennel;
- reserve your dog's place with KLM.

KLM requests that pet reservations be completed **at least 48 hours before departure**, although booking immediately after purchasing your ticket is strongly recommended because pet capacity is limited.

# Connecting through Amsterdam

Amsterdam Schiphol is one of Europe's largest transfer hubs. For the easiest experience, keep your itinerary entirely on KLM whenever possible, verify pet acceptance on all codeshare flights, and avoid long layovers if your dog travels in the hold. Because Air France and KLM share similar procedures, connections within the Air France–KLM Group are usually straightforward.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before reserving your pet

Pet capacity is limited on every flight.

## Choosing a kennel that is too large or too small

Both weight and external dimensions must comply with KLM's limits.

## Forgetting country-specific regulations

Import permits, veterinary certificates and quarantine rules remain the passenger's responsibility.

## Assuming partner airlines apply KLM rules

Codeshare flights may follow completely different pet policies.

# Expert advice

KLM is one of the best airlines in Europe for travelling with a dog. Its combination of flexible cabin transport, reliable hold facilities and the exceptional connectivity of Amsterdam Schiphol makes it suitable for almost every type of international journey. For the smoothest experience, reserve your dog's place immediately after booking your ticket and, whenever possible, remain on flights operated exclusively by KLM or Air France.

### Should I reserve early?

Yes. KLM recommends booking your pet **at least 48 hours before departure**, but earlier is always better because the number of animals accepted on each flight is limited.

### Do destination-country regulations still apply?

Always. KLM approval never replaces veterinary, customs or quarantine requirements imposed by the destination country.
