# Flying with a Dog on Air Europa

## Overview

Air Europa is Spain's third-largest airline and a member of the SkyTeam alliance. Operating primarily from Madrid-Barajas Airport, it connects Spain with Europe, North Africa, North America, Latin America and the Caribbean.

For travellers flying with a dog, Air Europa is particularly attractive thanks to its extensive network between Europe and Latin America. Madrid also serves as an efficient connecting hub for many international itineraries.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Air Europa's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

MyDogCanFly evaluates all three together before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Air Europa a good choice?

Yes. Air Europa is one of the strongest choices for travellers heading to Spain, Latin America or the Caribbean. Its principal strengths include an excellent long-haul network from Madrid, numerous direct routes to South America, and SkyTeam connectivity with other international airlines. If your itinerary can remain entirely on Air Europa-operated flights, travelling with your dog generally becomes simpler.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_air_europa | cabin_policy}}

Dogs and cats may travel in the cabin on many routes. The carrier must be soft-sided and fit under the seat. The pet may weigh up to **8 kg**, with a combined pet-and-carrier limit of **10 kg**. Business Class on Boeing 787 aircraft does not allow pets in the cabin because the carrier cannot fit under the seat.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_air_europa | hold_policy}}

Larger dogs may travel in the ventilated cargo hold inside an IATA-compliant crate. The combined weight of the pet and crate must not exceed **50 kg**; heavier animals are handled through Air Europa Cargo. Brachycephalic breeds are subject to additional restrictions in the hold.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- confirm your dog's travel weight;
- measure the carrier or crate;
- review destination-country import requirements.

Air Europa recommends arriving **3 hours before long-haul flights** (2 hours for domestic or medium-haul flights) when travelling with a pet, and pet transport must be added to the booking in advance.

# Madrid connections

Madrid-Barajas is Air Europa's primary hub. If another airline operates part of your itinerary, verify that every operating carrier accepts pets under compatible conditions. Whenever possible, avoid changing airline during the journey.

# Common mistakes

- Booking before reserving the pet.
- Choosing a rigid carrier for cabin travel instead of an approved flexible carrier.
- Forgetting destination-country veterinary requirements.
- Assuming Business Class always accepts pets in the cabin.

# Expert advice

Air Europa is one of the best airlines for travellers flying between Europe and Latin America with a dog. Whenever possible, choose an itinerary operated entirely by Air Europa. Fewer airline changes usually mean fewer administrative procedures and a smoother journey for both you and your dog.
