# Flying with a Dog on SAS Scandinavian Airlines

## Overview

SAS Scandinavian Airlines is the flag carrier of Denmark, Norway and Sweden. From its hubs at **Copenhagen (CPH), Stockholm Arlanda (ARN)** and **Oslo (OSL)**, SAS operates an extensive network throughout Europe, North America and Asia.

SAS welcomes companion dogs on most flights. Depending on your dog's size, pets may travel **inside the passenger cabin** or **inside the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. This makes SAS one of the most pet-friendly airlines in Northern Europe.

A successful journey always depends on three independent factors:

- SAS' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs regulations;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and pet capacity.

Yes. SAS is one of the best airlines in Northern Europe for travelling with a dog. Its main strengths include pets accepted in cabin and hold, an extensive Scandinavian network, long-haul destinations, a modern fleet, and a straightforward booking process. For travellers flying between Scandinavia and the rest of Europe, SAS is often among the best choices available.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_sas | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel in the passenger cabin inside an approved soft carrier placed beneath the seat. Acceptance depends on a maximum combined weight, carrier dimensions, and available pet capacity. Pets must remain inside the carrier throughout the flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_sas | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding cabin limits may travel in the aircraft's ventilated and temperature-controlled hold. The kennel must comply with **IATA Live Animals Regulations** and allow the dog to stand, turn around and lie comfortably. Acceptance depends on aircraft type and operational conditions.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_sas | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds and breeds presenting increased transport risks may be restricted. Always verify eligibility before booking.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify destination-country import requirements;
- ensure your dog's vaccinations are up to date;
- reserve your dog's place with SAS;
- prepare an IATA-compliant kennel if travelling in the hold.

Pet capacity is limited on every flight.

# Connecting flights

If your itinerary includes another airline, verify each airline's pet policy, confirm your dog's reservation for every segment, and allow sufficient transfer time.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before reserving your dog

Only a limited number of pets are accepted on each flight.

## Using a non-compliant kennel

Hold transport requires a kennel compliant with IATA standards.

## Forgetting destination-country regulations

Airline approval never replaces veterinary or customs requirements.

## Booking codeshare flights without checking

Partner airlines may apply different pet policies.

# Expert advice

SAS is one of the safest choices for travelling with a dog in Northern Europe. If your itinerary includes several airlines, verify every segment carefully, particularly on codeshare flights, where pet policies may differ.
