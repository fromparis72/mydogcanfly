# Flying with a Dog on China Airlines

## Overview

China Airlines is the flag carrier of Taiwan and a member of the **SkyTeam** alliance. From its main hub at **Taipei Taoyuan International Airport (TPE)**, the airline operates an extensive network throughout **Asia, Europe, North America and Oceania**.

China Airlines accepts companion dogs on many international routes. Pets normally travel **as checked animals in the aircraft's climate-controlled cargo hold**. Only recognised service dogs are permitted to remain in the passenger cabin. Some destinations or oversized animals require transport as manifest cargo instead.

As with every international trip, travelling with a dog depends on both:

- China Airlines' transport policy;
- the veterinary import requirements of the destination country.

MyDogCanFly analyses both before recommending an itinerary.

# Is China Airlines a good choice?

Yes. China Airlines has long experience transporting companion animals internationally and offers a structured process for pet travel. Its main strengths include an extensive intercontinental network, a modern Airbus A350 and Boeing 777 fleet, dedicated live-animal procedures, experienced handling teams, and strong SkyTeam connectivity. It is one of the best options for travellers flying between Taiwan and Europe or North America.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_china_airlines | cabin_policy}}

Companion dogs are **not normally accepted in the passenger cabin**. Only trained service dogs travelling with eligible passengers may accompany their handler in the cabin free of charge, subject to applicable regulations.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_china_airlines | hold_policy}}

Most companion dogs travel inside the aircraft's ventilated, pressurised and temperature-controlled cargo hold as checked animals. General requirements include advance reservation (at least 48 hours before departure); one pet per kennel; an IATA-compliant rigid kennel; valid veterinary documentation; and acceptance depending on aircraft type, route and local airport restrictions. Some aircraft types and certain destinations cannot accommodate checked pets.

Cargo transport: {{fait: airline_china_airlines | cargo_policy}}

Oversized animals or pets travelling to destinations requiring manifest cargo must be shipped through **China Airlines Cargo**. Cargo transport may also be required because of airport regulations or weight limitations.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_china_airlines | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds may be subject to operational restrictions because of increased respiratory risks. Additional seasonal temperature restrictions may also apply.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify your destination country's import requirements;
- ensure your dog's vaccinations and identification are compliant;
- contact China Airlines at least 48 hours before departure;
- prepare an IATA-approved kennel;
- confirm whether your itinerary allows transport as checked baggage or cargo.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before requesting pet transport

China Airlines requires advance approval and each flight accepts only a limited number of kennels.

## Assuming every aircraft accepts pets

Some aircraft types and routes cannot transport checked animals.

## Forgetting destination-country regulations

Airline approval never replaces customs, veterinary or quarantine requirements.

## Using a non-compliant kennel

Only kennels meeting IATA Live Animals Regulations are accepted.

# Expert advice

China Airlines is one of the strongest options for travelling with a dog between Taiwan and the rest of the world. If your itinerary includes connections or countries with stricter import rules, confirm every requirement before purchasing your ticket. Early planning is particularly important because some destinations require cargo transport instead of checked baggage.
