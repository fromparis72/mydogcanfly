# Flying with a Dog on Iberia

## Overview

Iberia is Spain's national airline and a member of the **oneworld Alliance**. Operating primarily from its hub at **Madrid–Barajas Airport (MAD)**, Iberia connects Europe with North America, Latin America, Africa and selected destinations in Asia.

For travellers flying with a dog, Iberia offers one of the most comprehensive pet programmes among European airlines. Depending on your dog's size, breed and destination, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin**, **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**, or, when required, via specialised cargo services.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Iberia's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, routing, weather and available pet capacity.

# Is Iberia a good choice?

Yes. Iberia is one of the strongest options for travellers flying between Europe and Latin America with a dog. Its main strengths include pets accepted in both cabin and hold, an excellent long-haul network to Central and South America, an efficient hub in Madrid, a straightforward booking process, and a modern long-haul fleet. For travellers departing from Southern Europe, Iberia is often one of the easiest airlines for travelling with a dog.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_iberia | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel in the passenger cabin inside an approved carrier placed beneath the seat. Cabin transport is subject to weight limits, carrier dimensions and the maximum number of pets accepted on each flight. Pets must remain inside their carrier throughout the journey.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_iberia | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding cabin limits may travel inside the aircraft's climate-controlled hold in an IATA-compliant kennel. The kennel must allow the dog to stand naturally, turn around and lie comfortably. Depending on destination-country regulations, some animals may be required to travel via cargo.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_iberia | breed_restrictions}}

Additional restrictions may apply to brachycephalic breeds and certain powerful breeds. Always verify eligibility before purchasing your ticket.

# Before booking

Before confirming your reservation:

- verify destination-country import requirements;
- ensure your dog is microchipped and vaccinated;
- select an IATA-compliant carrier or kennel;
- reserve your dog's place with Iberia.

Pet transport is subject to availability on every flight.

# Connecting through Madrid

Madrid–Barajas is one of Europe's largest international hubs. For the smoothest experience, remain on Iberia-operated flights whenever possible, verify every codeshare segment, and avoid short connections if your dog travels in the hold. Partner airlines may apply different pet policies.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before reserving pet space

Pet capacity is limited on every aircraft.

## Using a non-compliant kennel

Only IATA-compliant kennels are accepted for hold transport.

## Forgetting destination-country regulations

Airline approval never replaces veterinary or customs requirements.

## Booking partner flights without checking

Each airline applies its own pet transport policy.

# Expert advice

Iberia is one of Europe's best airlines for travelling with a dog, especially between Europe and Latin America. Its combination of cabin transport, climate-controlled hold facilities and Madrid's excellent connectivity makes it suitable for both leisure and long-haul international travel. Whenever possible, keep your itinerary entirely on Iberia-operated flights to simplify connections.
