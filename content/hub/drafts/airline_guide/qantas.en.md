# Flying with a Dog on Qantas

## Overview

Qantas is Australia's flag carrier and one of the world's longest-established airlines. Operating from major hubs in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Perth, it connects Australia with Asia, Europe, North America, New Zealand and the Pacific.

For travellers flying with a dog, Qantas follows a model that differs significantly from many North American and European airlines. **Pet dogs and cats are not accepted in the passenger cabin**, except for accredited assistance dogs. Companion animals travel through **Qantas Freight**, using specialised live-animal transport procedures.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Qantas' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and freight availability.

# Is Qantas a good choice?

Yes. Qantas has decades of experience transporting animals throughout Australia. Its strengths include Australia's largest domestic network, an extensive international network, dedicated Qantas Freight animal services, and experienced live-animal handling teams. Unlike airlines that check pets as passenger baggage, Qantas manages most companion animals through its freight division.

# Cabin, hold or freight

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_qantas | cabin_policy}}

Only accredited Guide, Hearing and Assistance Dogs are permitted inside the passenger cabin. Emotional support animals are not accepted as cabin pets.

Freight transport: {{fait: airline_qantas | hold_policy}}

Pet dogs and cats travel via **Qantas Freight**, in an IATA-compliant travel crate inside a temperature-controlled section of the aircraft. Owners are encouraged to reserve the pet's journey **before booking their own ticket**, because not every flight can accommodate animals.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_qantas | breed_restrictions}}

Additional weather restrictions and breed-specific limitations may apply depending on the route and season.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify that Qantas Freight serves your route;
- book your dog's transport before purchasing your own ticket whenever possible;
- prepare an IATA-compliant crate;
- review quarantine and import requirements.

For international travel, Qantas strongly recommends using a professional pet relocation specialist because customs and quarantine procedures can be complex.

# Connections

Whenever possible, keep your itinerary entirely on Qantas-operated services. If another airline is involved, verify that its transport method (checked baggage versus freight) is compatible with Qantas Freight procedures.

# Common mistakes

- Assuming pets may travel in the passenger cabin.
- Booking your own ticket before confirming pet availability.
- Underestimating Australian quarantine regulations.
- Using a travel crate that does not comply with IATA standards.

# Expert advice

Qantas is one of the world's most experienced airlines for transporting animals, but its process differs substantially from European carriers. For international travel, working with an experienced pet relocation company is often the easiest way to manage veterinary paperwork, freight booking and quarantine requirements.

### Should I book my dog's transport before my own ticket?

Yes. Qantas specifically recommends securing your pet's transport before booking your own flight because animal capacity is limited on eligible services.

### Do Australian import rules still apply?

Always. Qantas' transport approval never replaces Australian or destination-country veterinary, customs and quarantine regulations.
