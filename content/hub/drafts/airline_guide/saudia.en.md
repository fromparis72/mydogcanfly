# Flying with a Dog on Saudia
## Overview
Saudia is the national airline of Saudi Arabia and a member of the **SkyTeam** alliance. Operating from its hubs in **Jeddah (JED)**, **Riyadh (RUH)** and **Dammam (DMM)**, the airline connects the Middle East with Europe, Asia, Africa and North America.
Saudia accepts pets on selected routes, although transportation is subject to operational restrictions and Saudi Arabian import regulations. Depending on your itinerary, dogs generally travel **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**, while oversized animals or specific destinations may require transport as cargo. Only recognised assistance dogs may be accepted in the passenger cabin where regulations permit.

Yes, provided your destination accepts pet imports and your itinerary meets the airline's operational requirements.
Saudia is particularly relevant for travellers flying between Europe, the Middle East and Asia. However, because Saudi Arabia applies strict veterinary regulations and some routes have additional operational constraints, preparation is more important than with many European airlines.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify Saudi Arabian import or transit requirements;
- confirm that pets are accepted on your specific route;
- reserve your dog's transport well in advance;
- prepare all veterinary documentation;
- ensure your kennel complies with IATA Live Animals Regulations.

# Common Mistakes
## Assuming every Saudia route accepts pets
Acceptance depends on aircraft type, destination and local regulations.
---
## Underestimating Saudi veterinary requirements
Import rules may be considerably stricter than those of your departure country.
---
## Booking too late
Pet capacity is limited on every flight.
---
## Using a non-compliant kennel
Only IATA-approved kennels are accepted.

# Expert Advice
Saudia is a reliable airline for travellers heading to or through Saudi Arabia, but administrative preparation is essential. Confirm airline approval, destination requirements and veterinary documentation before booking non-refundable travel arrangements.
