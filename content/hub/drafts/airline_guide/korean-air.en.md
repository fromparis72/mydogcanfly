# Flying with a Dog on Korean Air

## Overview

Korean Air is South Korea's flag carrier and a member of the **SkyTeam** alliance. From its hubs at **Seoul Incheon (ICN)** and **Seoul Gimpo (GMP)**, the airline serves an extensive network across Asia, Europe, North America, Oceania and the Middle East.

Korean Air accepts companion dogs on most flights. Depending on their size, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Animals exceeding the airline's operational limits may have to travel as cargo.

Travelling with a dog to or from South Korea also requires compliance with the veterinary import regulations of the destination country, which are separate from the airline's own acceptance policy.

Yes. Korean Air is one of Asia's leading airlines for travelling with pets. Its main strengths include pets accepted in cabin and hold, an extensive international network, a modern long-haul fleet, dedicated live-animal handling procedures, and excellent operational reliability. For travellers flying to or from South Korea, Korean Air is among the safest and most practical options.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_korean_air | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel in the passenger cabin inside an approved soft carrier placed under the seat. Cabin transport depends on a maximum combined weight, carrier dimensions, and available pet capacity. Pets must remain inside the carrier throughout the flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_korean_air | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding cabin limits may travel inside the aircraft's ventilated, pressurised and temperature-controlled hold. Requirements include an IATA-compliant rigid kennel; advance reservation; valid veterinary documentation; and operational acceptance according to aircraft type.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_korean_air | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds may be subject to transport restrictions because of increased respiratory risks. Seasonal embargoes may apply during periods of extreme temperatures.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify destination-country import requirements;
- ensure your dog's vaccinations are up to date;
- reserve pet transport with Korean Air;
- prepare an IATA-compliant kennel if travelling in the hold.

International destinations may require additional veterinary certificates or import permits.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before reserving your pet

Pet capacity is limited on every flight.

## Using a non-compliant kennel

Only kennels meeting IATA standards are accepted for hold transport.

## Forgetting destination-country regulations

Airline approval never replaces veterinary or customs requirements.

## Travelling during extreme weather

Heat or cold restrictions may temporarily affect pet transport.

# Expert advice

Korean Air is an excellent choice for travelling with a dog between Asia, Europe and North America. Book your dog's transport as early as possible and verify the import requirements of your destination well in advance, particularly for international flights.
