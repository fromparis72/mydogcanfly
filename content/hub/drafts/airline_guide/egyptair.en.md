# Flying with a Dog on EgyptAir
## Overview
EgyptAir is the national airline of Egypt and a member of the **Star Alliance**. From its main hub at **Cairo International Airport (CAI)**, the airline serves destinations across **Africa, Europe, the Middle East, Asia and North America**.
EgyptAir accepts pets on selected domestic and international routes. Depending on your dog's size and the applicable regulations, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Oversized animals or certain destinations may require transport as cargo.
MyDogCanFly analyses both EgyptAir's pet policy and your destination country's veterinary regulations before recommending an itinerary.

# Is EgyptAir a good choice?
Yes.
EgyptAir is a practical option for travelling with a dog between Europe, Africa and the Middle East. Its Star Alliance membership and extensive regional network make it particularly useful for connecting itineraries.
However, pet transport conditions and import regulations should always be confirmed before booking.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify Egypt's or your destination's import requirements;
- reserve pet transport as early as possible;
- ensure your kennel complies with IATA standards;
- prepare all veterinary documentation;
- confirm pet acceptance for your specific aircraft and route.
---
# Common Mistakes
## Assuming every route accepts pets
Acceptance varies according to aircraft type and destination.
---
## Waiting too long before requesting approval
Pet capacity is limited on every flight.
---
## Forgetting veterinary documentation
Official certificates remain mandatory even if the airline accepts your dog.
---
## Using a non-compliant kennel
Only IATA-approved kennels are accepted for hold transport.

# Expert Advice
EgyptAir is particularly useful for travellers connecting Europe, Africa and the Middle East.
Always verify your dog's reservation before purchasing additional travel services, and pay special attention to veterinary import requirements if Egypt is your final destination.
