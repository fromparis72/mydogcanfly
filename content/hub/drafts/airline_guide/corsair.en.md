# Flying with a Dog on Corsair

## Overview

Corsair is a French airline specializing in long-haul services between mainland France, the French overseas territories, Africa and the Indian Ocean. From its main base at **Paris-Orly Airport (ORY)**, it serves destinations including **Guadeloupe, Martinique, Réunion, Mayotte, Madagascar, Côte d'Ivoire** and **Mauritius**.

For travellers flying with a dog, Corsair offers one of the most flexible pet policies among French long-haul airlines. Depending on your dog's size, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Animals exceeding the maximum permitted weight must travel as cargo.

A successful journey depends on three independent factors:

- Corsair's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs regulations;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather and available pet capacity.

# Is Corsair a good choice?

Yes. Corsair is particularly attractive for travellers flying to French overseas destinations with their dog. Its main strengths include pets accepted in both cabin and hold, long-haul expertise, excellent coverage of French overseas territories, and dedicated procedures for pet transport. For French travellers heading to Martinique, Guadeloupe or Réunion, Corsair is one of the leading options.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_corsair | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel inside the passenger cabin in an approved carrier placed beneath the seat. Current policy includes one pet per passenger; advance reservation required; pets must remain inside the carrier throughout the flight; and pets are not accepted in the cabin on routes to or from **Mauritius**.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_corsair | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding the cabin weight limit must travel in the aircraft's ventilated and temperature-controlled hold. The kennel must comply with **IATA Live Animals Regulations**. Current policy also states a maximum combined weight (dog + kennel) of **50 kg**; above 50 kg, transport must be arranged as cargo; and veterinary health documentation is required before departure.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_corsair | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds are not accepted in the hold because of increased respiratory risks. French Category 1 dogs are prohibited, while some Category 2 dogs may travel subject to strict legal requirements and documentation.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify destination-country veterinary requirements;
- ensure your dog is microchipped and vaccinated;
- reserve pet transport **at least 72 hours before departure**;
- prepare an IATA-compliant kennel if travelling in the hold.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before reserving your dog

Corsair requires advance reservation and pet capacity is limited.

## Forgetting destination-specific restrictions

Some destinations—such as Mauritius—apply additional rules regarding pets travelling in the cabin.

## Using a non-compliant kennel

Hold transport requires an IATA-approved rigid kennel.

## Ignoring veterinary paperwork

Health certificates and vaccination requirements vary depending on the destination country.

# Expert advice

Corsair is one of the best airlines for travelling with a dog between mainland France and the French overseas territories. Because several destinations apply additional veterinary rules, we recommend preparing your paperwork well in advance and reserving your dog's place as soon as you book your ticket.
