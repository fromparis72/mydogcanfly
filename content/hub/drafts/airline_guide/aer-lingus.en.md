# Flying with a Dog on Aer Lingus
## Overview
Aer Lingus is the flag carrier of Ireland. From its main hub at Dublin (DUB), it connects Ireland with Europe and North America.
Aer Lingus carries pets as cargo through its cargo service; only recognised assistance dogs travel in the passenger cabin.
A successful journey depends on three things: the airline's transport policy, the destination country's veterinary requirements, and the operational limits of your specific route. MyDogCanFly checks all three before recommending an itinerary.

Yes, provided your destination accepts pet imports and your itinerary meets the airline's operational requirements. Aer Lingus is especially relevant for journeys involving Europe and North America. As always, confirm both airline approval and the destination country's veterinary rules before booking.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify the destination country's import requirements;
- confirm that pets are accepted on your specific route;
- reserve your dog's transport as early as possible;
- prepare all veterinary documentation;
- ensure your kennel complies with IATA Live Animals Regulations.
---
# Common Mistakes
## Booking too late
Pet capacity is limited on every flight.
---
## Underestimating destination requirements
Import rules can be far stricter than those of your departure country.
---
## Using a non-compliant kennel
Only IATA-approved kennels are accepted.
---
## Forgetting transit-country rules
Some itineraries cross countries with their own animal import or transit rules.
---
# Expert Advice
Aer Lingus can be a reliable choice for journeys involving Europe and North America, but administrative preparation matters more than the flight itself. Confirm airline approval, destination requirements and veterinary documents before booking non-refundable arrangements.
