# Flying with a Dog on United Airlines

## Overview

United Airlines is one of the largest airlines in the world and a founding member of the **Star Alliance**. From its major hubs in **Chicago (ORD), Denver (DEN), Houston (IAH), Newark (EWR), San Francisco (SFO), Washington Dulles (IAD), Los Angeles (LAX)** and **Guam (GUM)**, United serves hundreds of destinations across North America, Europe, Asia, Oceania and Latin America.

For dog owners, United's policy differs from many European airlines. **Small dogs may travel in the passenger cabin**, while traditional accompanied checked-pet transport is generally no longer available. Larger dogs usually require transport through specialised cargo or pet relocation services, depending on the itinerary and current operational rules.

A successful journey always depends on three independent factors:

- United Airlines' transport policy;
- veterinary and customs requirements of the destination country;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

# Is United Airlines a good choice?

Yes, if your dog qualifies for cabin travel. United offers one of the largest domestic U.S. networks, extensive international coverage, Star Alliance connectivity, and a well-established in-cabin pet programme. However, unlike many European airlines, United generally **does not transport large companion dogs as checked baggage**, making it less suitable for owners of large breeds.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_united | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel in an airline-approved carrier placed completely beneath the seat in front of you. Only a limited number of pets are accepted on each aircraft, so reservations should be completed as early as possible. Pets must remain inside their carrier throughout the flight.

Cargo transport: {{fait: airline_united | hold_policy}}

United generally no longer offers traditional checked-baggage transport for companion pets. Large dogs usually require specialised cargo arrangements or professional pet relocation services depending on the route and current eligibility rules. Some military exceptions may apply.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_united | breed_restrictions}}

Additional operational restrictions may apply to certain breeds, destination countries or weather conditions. Always verify eligibility before purchasing your ticket.

# Before booking

Before confirming your reservation:

- verify destination-country import requirements;
- ensure your dog's vaccinations and identification comply with local regulations;
- reserve your dog's place immediately;
- prepare an airline-approved carrier.

International itineraries may require additional veterinary documentation depending on your destination.

# Connecting through the United States

United operates some of the busiest hubs in North America. Whenever possible, remain on United-operated flights, verify pet acceptance on every partner airline, and allow sufficient connection time. Codeshare flights may follow different pet policies.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before reserving cabin space

Pet capacity is limited on every flight.

## Assuming large dogs can travel as checked baggage

For most passengers, this option is no longer available.

## Forgetting U.S. import requirements

Dogs entering or re-entering the United States may require additional documentation depending on current CDC regulations.

## Booking partner flights without checking

Each airline applies its own animal transport policy.

# Expert advice

United is one of the best choices for travelling with a **small dog** throughout North America. For larger dogs, however, alternative airlines that still offer accompanied hold transport may provide a much simpler travel experience. Whenever possible, keep your itinerary entirely on United-operated flights and reserve your pet immediately after purchasing your ticket.
