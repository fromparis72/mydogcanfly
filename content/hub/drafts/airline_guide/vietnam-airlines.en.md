# Flying with a Dog on Vietnam Airlines

## Overview

Vietnam Airlines is Vietnam's national airline and a member of the **SkyTeam** alliance. From its hubs at **Hanoi (HAN)** and **Ho Chi Minh City (SGN)**, the airline serves destinations throughout **Asia, Europe and Australia**.

Vietnam Airlines accepts companion dogs on many domestic and international flights. Depending on their size and the route, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Animals exceeding operational limits may be transported as cargo.

A successful journey always depends on three independent factors:

- Vietnam Airlines' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs regulations;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

# Is Vietnam Airlines a good choice?

Yes. Vietnam Airlines offers one of the most comprehensive pet transport services in Southeast Asia. Its main strengths include pets accepted in cabin and hold, an extensive domestic Vietnamese network, a growing international network, a modern long-haul fleet, and dedicated live-animal handling procedures. For travellers flying to or from Vietnam, it is one of the best available options.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_vietnam_airlines | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel inside the passenger cabin in an approved carrier placed beneath the seat. Acceptance depends on applicable weight limits, carrier dimensions, available pet capacity, and destination regulations. Pets must remain inside the carrier throughout the flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_vietnam_airlines | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding the cabin limits travel inside the aircraft's ventilated, pressurised and temperature-controlled hold. Requirements generally include advance reservation; an IATA-compliant rigid kennel; valid veterinary documentation; and operational acceptance according to aircraft type. Oversized animals or specific destinations may require cargo transport.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_vietnam_airlines | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds may be subject to additional operational restrictions because of increased respiratory risks. Seasonal embargoes may also apply during periods of extreme temperatures.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify destination-country import requirements;
- ensure your dog's vaccinations are up to date;
- reserve pet transport with Vietnam Airlines;
- prepare an IATA-compliant kennel if travelling in the hold.

Some countries require import permits, veterinary certificates or quarantine procedures.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before reserving your pet

Pet capacity is limited on every flight.

## Using a kennel that does not comply with IATA standards

Only compliant kennels are accepted for hold transport.

## Forgetting destination-country regulations

Airline approval never replaces veterinary or customs requirements.

## Assuming every route accepts pets

Operational restrictions may vary according to aircraft type and destination.

# Expert advice

Vietnam Airlines is one of the strongest choices for travelling with a dog within Southeast Asia and on routes connecting Vietnam with Europe or Australia. Always confirm your dog's reservation before departure and prepare veterinary documentation well in advance.
