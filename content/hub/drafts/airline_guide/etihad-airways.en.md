# Flying with a Dog on Etihad Airways

## Overview

Etihad Airways is the national airline of the United Arab Emirates. Operating from its hub at Abu Dhabi International Airport (AUH), it connects Europe, Asia, Africa, Australia and North America through one of the world's fastest-growing international networks.

Unlike many Middle Eastern carriers, **Etihad allows eligible cats and dogs to travel in the passenger cabin on many routes**, making it one of the few long-haul airlines offering this option. Larger pets may still require specialised cargo transport depending on their size and destination.

A successful journey always depends on three independent elements:

- Etihad Airways' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

# Is Etihad Airways a good choice?

Yes. Etihad has become one of the most pet-friendly major long-haul airlines thanks to its cabin pet programme. Its principal strengths include pets accepted in the cabin on eligible routes, a modern wide-body fleet, a premium onboard experience, and excellent connectivity through Abu Dhabi. For owners of small dogs, Etihad can be an excellent alternative to airlines that only permit cargo transport.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_etihad | cabin_policy}}

Eligible dogs and cats may travel in Economy or Business Class when the pet is at least **16 weeks old**; the combined weight of the pet and carrier does not exceed **8 kg**; the reservation request is submitted at least **7 days before departure**; and all required veterinary documents are received at least **72 hours before departure**.

Hold / cargo transport: {{fait: airline_etihad | hold_policy}}

Larger dogs generally travel via specialised cargo arrangements depending on the itinerary and destination-country regulations.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_etihad | breed_restrictions}}

Certain destinations and some brachycephalic breeds may be subject to additional operational restrictions.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify that your route accepts pets;
- complete Etihad's booking request form;
- prepare veterinary certificates;
- verify destination-country import permits;
- confirm your carrier dimensions.

Etihad requires the booking request at least **7 days before travel**, followed by document submission **72 hours before departure**.

# Abu Dhabi connections

Abu Dhabi is designed as a major international transfer hub. If your itinerary combines Etihad with another airline, verify that every operating carrier accepts pets under compatible rules. Remember that some partner airlines may require cargo transport even when Etihad accepts cabin pets.

# Common mistakes

- Booking flights before obtaining pet approval.
- Missing the 7-day booking deadline.
- Sending veterinary documents too late.
- Forgetting destination-country import permits.

# Expert advice

Etihad is currently one of the few premium intercontinental airlines that allows small dogs to travel in the passenger cabin on many routes. If your dog meets the weight requirements, this can provide a significantly more comfortable journey than cargo transport. Always start the booking process early because veterinary documentation is carefully reviewed before approval.
