# Flying with a Dog on Ethiopian Airlines
## Overview
Ethiopian Airlines is the national airline of Ethiopia and Africa's largest carrier. As a member of the **Star Alliance**, it operates an extensive global network from its main hub at **Addis Ababa Bole International Airport (ADD)**, connecting Africa with Europe, Asia, the Middle East and the Americas.
Ethiopian Airlines accepts companion animals on many international routes. Depending on your dog's size, destination and operational requirements, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin**, **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**, or **as manifest cargo**.
MyDogCanFly analyses both Ethiopian Airlines' transport policy and your destination country's veterinary requirements before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Ethiopian Airlines a good choice?
Yes.
Ethiopian Airlines is one of the strongest choices for travellers flying between Africa and the rest of the world with a dog. Its extensive network and long experience transporting live animals make it particularly attractive for complex international itineraries.
As with any international journey, airline approval and destination-country veterinary regulations must both be satisfied.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify your destination's import requirements;
- reserve pet transport as early as possible;
- prepare all veterinary documentation;
- ensure your kennel complies with IATA standards;
- confirm pet acceptance for your itinerary.
---
# Common Mistakes
## Waiting too long before requesting pet transport
Pet capacity is limited.
---
## Assuming every destination follows the same rules
Veterinary regulations vary considerably from one country to another.
---
## Using a non-compliant kennel
Only IATA-approved kennels are accepted.
---
## Forgetting transit-country requirements
Some itineraries involve countries with their own animal import or transit rules.
---
# Expert Advice
Ethiopian Airlines is particularly well suited for long-haul journeys involving Africa thanks to its extensive network.
If your itinerary includes one or more stopovers, verify the veterinary requirements of every country involved—not only your final destination.
