# Flying with a Dog on Emirates

## Overview

Emirates is one of the world's largest international airlines, serving more than 140 destinations across six continents from its global hub at Dubai International Airport (DXB).

For travellers flying with a dog, Emirates follows a transport model that differs from most European airlines. **Companion pets are generally not accepted in the passenger cabin**, except for recognised assistance dogs travelling on eligible routes. Most dogs travel through **Emirates SkyCargo – Emirates Pets**, one of the world's largest live-animal transport services.

Its extensive long-haul network makes Emirates a major choice for journeys between Europe, Asia, Africa, Oceania and the Americas.

A successful trip depends on three independent elements:

- Emirates' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and cargo availability.

# Is Emirates a good choice?

Yes. Emirates is considered one of the world's leading airlines for international pet transport thanks to its dedicated cargo facilities in Dubai and its extensive experience handling live animals. Its strengths include one of the largest global route networks, dedicated Emirates SkyCargo animal facilities, a modern wide-body fleet, excellent operational reliability, and worldwide connectivity through Dubai. For intercontinental travel, Emirates is frequently one of the strongest options.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_emirates | cabin_policy}}

With very limited exceptions for recognised assistance dogs, companion dogs are **not permitted in the passenger cabin**.

Cargo transport: {{fait: airline_emirates | hold_policy}}

Most companion dogs travel via **Emirates SkyCargo** inside an IATA-compliant travel crate. Depending on the origin and destination, pets may travel as manifested cargo rather than checked baggage. Owners should verify the applicable transport method before purchasing tickets.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_emirates | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds and temperature-sensitive animals may be subject to seasonal restrictions or additional operational requirements.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify the transport method required for your route;
- prepare an IATA-compliant crate;
- obtain veterinary certificates and import permits;
- contact Emirates or an approved pet relocation company.

For many international routes, professional pet shippers can coordinate veterinary documentation, customs clearance and cargo reservations.

# Dubai connections

Dubai International Airport is one of the world's busiest aviation hubs. If your itinerary combines Emirates with another airline, verify that both carriers use compatible transport procedures. Some airlines transport pets as checked baggage while Emirates may require cargo transport.

# Common mistakes

- Assuming pets can travel in the passenger cabin.
- Booking flights before confirming cargo availability.
- Underestimating destination-country import requirements.
- Using a crate that does not comply with IATA standards.

# Expert advice

Emirates is an excellent airline for intercontinental pet transport. For complex international journeys, especially those involving quarantine countries or multiple permits, using an experienced pet relocation specialist will usually save significant time and reduce administrative risks.
