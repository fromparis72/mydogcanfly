# Flying with a Dog on Air India

## Overview

Air India is the flag carrier of India and a member of the **Star Alliance**. From its major hubs at **Delhi (DEL)**, **Mumbai (BOM)**, **Bengaluru (BLR)** and **Hyderabad (HYD)**, the airline serves an extensive network across **Asia, Europe, North America, Australia and the Middle East**.

Air India accepts companion animals on selected domestic and international flights. Depending on the route, aircraft type and applicable regulations, dogs may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Larger animals or destinations with specific requirements may require transport as cargo.

As with every international trip, travelling with a dog depends both on Air India's transport policy and on the veterinary import requirements of the destination country.

# Is Air India a good choice?

Yes. Following its modernization programme, Air India has continued improving its services, including procedures relating to the transport of companion animals. Its main strengths include pets accepted on many domestic and international routes, an extensive Indian domestic network, a growing long-haul network, Star Alliance connectivity, and dedicated live-animal handling. For travellers flying to or from India, Air India is one of the most practical options.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_air_india | cabin_policy}}

Depending on the route and applicable regulations, small dogs may travel in the passenger cabin inside an approved carrier placed beneath the seat. Acceptance depends on operational limitations, available pet capacity, carrier dimensions, and applicable veterinary regulations. Pets must remain inside the carrier throughout the flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_air_india | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding cabin limits travel inside the aircraft's ventilated, pressurised and temperature-controlled hold. Requirements generally include advance reservation; an IATA-compliant rigid kennel; valid veterinary documentation; and acceptance according to aircraft type and destination. Oversized animals may require cargo transport.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_air_india | breed_restrictions}}

Certain breeds may be subject to operational restrictions, particularly brachycephalic dogs. Temporary weather embargoes may also apply.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify destination-country import requirements;
- ensure your dog's vaccinations and identification are compliant;
- reserve pet transport with Air India;
- prepare an IATA-compliant kennel if travelling in the hold.

Some destinations require import permits, official health certificates or quarantine procedures.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before booking

Pet capacity is limited.

## Assuming every route accepts pets

Acceptance may vary depending on aircraft type and destination.

## Using a non-compliant kennel

Only IATA-compliant kennels are accepted.

## Forgetting destination-country regulations

Airline approval never replaces veterinary or customs requirements.

# Expert advice

Air India is a practical choice for travelling with a dog throughout India and on many international routes. Because pet acceptance may vary depending on the aircraft and destination, confirm your dog's reservation before purchasing non-refundable services and prepare all veterinary documentation well in advance.
