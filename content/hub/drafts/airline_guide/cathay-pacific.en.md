# Flying with a Dog on Cathay Pacific

## Overview

Cathay Pacific is Hong Kong's flag carrier and one of Asia's leading premium airlines. From its global hub at Hong Kong International Airport, it serves destinations across Asia, Europe, North America, Australia, New Zealand, Africa and the Middle East.

For travellers flying with a dog, Cathay Pacific operates under a model that differs from many European and North American airlines. **Pets are generally not accepted in the passenger cabin**, except for recognised assistance dogs. Most dogs travel through **Cathay Cargo's "Cathay Live Animal" service**, using professional live-animal handling procedures.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Cathay Pacific's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and cargo availability.

# Is Cathay Pacific a good choice?

Yes. Cathay Pacific has an excellent reputation for transporting live animals internationally. Unlike airlines that simply carry pets as checked baggage, Cathay relies on specialised cargo procedures and dedicated handling teams trained according to IATA Live Animals Regulations. This approach requires additional preparation but offers a highly professional transport process for international pet travel.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_cathay_pacific | cabin_policy}}

Only recognised assistance dogs are accepted inside the passenger cabin. Pets are otherwise not permitted in the cabin.

Cargo transport: {{fait: airline_cathay_pacific | hold_policy}}

Most dogs travel via **Cathay Cargo – Cathay Live Animal**, using IATA-compliant travel kennels and specialised live-animal procedures. Since September 2025, cargo bookings are accepted only through qualified pet transport professionals or approved freight forwarders.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_cathay_pacific | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic (snub-nosed) breeds are accepted only during cooler months and on selected routes under specific conditions.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify import requirements;
- contact an approved pet relocation company if required;
- prepare an IATA-compliant crate;
- obtain all veterinary certificates and permits.

Many countries served by Cathay Pacific—including Hong Kong, Australia and the United Kingdom—have strict import procedures that require advance planning.

# Hong Kong connections

Hong Kong International Airport is one of Asia's largest cargo hubs. If your itinerary includes another airline, verify that every carrier accepts live animals under compatible procedures. Some airlines transport pets as checked baggage, while Cathay may require cargo processing instead.

# Common mistakes

- Assuming pets may travel in the passenger cabin.
- Waiting too long before contacting a pet transport specialist.
- Underestimating Hong Kong import regulations.
- Using a crate that does not comply with IATA Live Animals Regulations.

# Expert advice

Cathay Pacific is one of Asia's reference airlines for international animal transport. If your dog is travelling on a long-haul international itinerary, working with an experienced IPATA-certified pet relocation company will usually make the process significantly smoother and reduce administrative risks.
