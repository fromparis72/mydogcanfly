# Flying with a Dog on SWISS

## Overview

SWISS International Air Lines is Switzerland's national carrier and a member of the Lufthansa Group and Star Alliance. Operating primarily from **Zurich (ZRH)** and **Geneva (GVA)**, SWISS connects Europe with North America, South America, Africa, the Middle East and Asia.

For travellers flying with a dog, SWISS is regarded as one of Europe's most reliable airlines. Depending on your dog's size and destination, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin**, **in the climate-controlled hold**, or, in exceptional situations, via Lufthansa Cargo.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- SWISS' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

MyDogCanFly evaluates all three before recommending an itinerary.

# Is SWISS a good choice?

Yes. SWISS consistently ranks among Europe's highest-quality airlines and offers one of the most structured pet transport programmes. Its strengths include pets accepted in both cabin and hold, premium service standards, excellent connections through Zurich, integration with the Lufthansa Group network, and strong operational reliability. For international travel, SWISS is frequently one of the safest choices for travelling with a dog.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_swiss | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs and cats may travel inside the passenger cabin in an approved soft carrier placed beneath the seat. Cabin transport is limited by weight, carrier dimensions and available pet capacity.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_swiss | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding cabin limits travel inside a ventilated and temperature-controlled hold using an IATA-compliant kennel. For certain oversized animals or special itineraries, Lufthansa Cargo may be required.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_swiss | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds and a limited number of powerful breeds may be subject to operational restrictions.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- measure your carrier or kennel;
- review destination-country regulations;
- request pet approval from SWISS.

Pet reservations should be completed as early as possible because each aircraft accepts only a limited number of animals.

# Zurich connections

Zurich is one of Europe's most efficient connecting airports. Whenever possible, remain on SWISS-operated flights, avoid very short connections if your dog travels in the hold, and verify pet acceptance on codeshare flights.

# Common mistakes

- Booking flights before reserving pet space.
- Using a kennel that is too small.
- Forgetting destination-country veterinary regulations.
- Assuming every Star Alliance airline follows SWISS rules.

# Expert advice

SWISS combines premium service with a highly structured pet policy. For long-haul journeys, travelling entirely on SWISS generally provides one of the smoothest experiences available in Europe, particularly when connecting through Zurich.
