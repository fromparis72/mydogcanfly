# Flying with a Dog on Qatar Airways

## Overview

Qatar Airways is one of the world's leading international airlines, operating an extensive network from its hub at **Hamad International Airport (DOH)** in Doha. It connects more than 170 destinations across Europe, Asia, Africa, Oceania and the Americas.

For travellers flying with a dog, Qatar Airways offers more flexibility than many long-haul carriers. Depending on the route, destination and the size of the animal, dogs may travel as **checked baggage** or as **manifest cargo**. Only recognised service dogs are permitted in the passenger cabin, while falcons have their own dedicated cabin policy.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Qatar Airways' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

# Is Qatar Airways a good choice?

Yes. Qatar Airways is one of the world's largest long-haul airlines and has extensive experience transporting companion animals internationally. Its main strengths include an exceptional worldwide network, one of the world's best transfer hubs, pets accepted as checked baggage on many routes, and specialised Qatar Airways Cargo services for larger or restricted shipments. Unlike some airlines that transport nearly all pets as cargo, Qatar Airways still accepts many cats and dogs as checked baggage when route, weight and destination regulations permit.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_qatar_airways | cabin_policy}}

Only recognised service dogs are accepted inside the passenger cabin. Companion dogs are not permitted in the cabin. Falcons may travel in Economy Class under separate rules.

Checked baggage: {{fait: airline_qatar_airways | hold_policy}}

Dogs, cats and birds may travel as checked baggage on many routes. Animals weighing up to **32 kg** (including kennel) are generally transported under the standard checked-pet policy, while heavier animals (up to **75 kg**) follow a higher fee category. Pets above **75 kg**, oversized kennels or certain destinations must travel as cargo. Australia and New Zealand require cargo transport regardless of weight.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_qatar_airways | breed_restrictions}}

Snub-nosed (brachycephalic) breeds are subject to additional restrictions and some may not be accepted because of increased respiratory risk during air transport.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify whether your route allows checked baggage or requires cargo;
- submit your pet request;
- prepare an IATA-compliant kennel;
- obtain veterinary certificates and import permits.

Qatar Airways asks passengers to notify the airline **at least 48 hours before departure** to confirm pet acceptance and space availability. A dedicated online request form is available for checked pets.

# Doha connections

Doha is one of the world's largest international hubs. If another airline operates part of your itinerary, verify that every carrier uses compatible pet transport procedures. Some airlines transport pets only as cargo, while Qatar Airways may allow checked baggage on the same journey.

# Common mistakes

- Assuming companion dogs may travel in the passenger cabin.
- Booking before confirming pet acceptance.
- Forgetting country-specific import permits.
- Using a kennel that does not comply with IATA Live Animals Regulations.

# Expert advice

Qatar Airways is one of the strongest choices for long-haul international pet travel. If your itinerary qualifies for checked baggage instead of cargo, the journey is usually simpler. However, for destinations such as Australia or New Zealand, cargo transport is mandatory regardless of your dog's size, so planning well in advance is essential.
