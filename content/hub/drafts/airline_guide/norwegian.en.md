# Flying with a Dog on Norwegian

## Overview

Norwegian is one of Scandinavia's leading low-cost airlines, operating an extensive network from **Oslo (OSL), Bergen (BGO), Stockholm Arlanda (ARN), Copenhagen (CPH), Helsinki (HEL)** and several European bases. The airline serves more than 100 destinations across Europe and selected international routes.

Unlike many European low-cost airlines, **Norwegian accepts companion dogs**. Depending on your dog's size, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. This makes Norwegian one of the few low-cost airlines genuinely suitable for travelling with a dog.

A successful journey always depends on three independent factors:

- Norwegian's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs regulations;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

# Is Norwegian a good choice?

Yes. Norwegian is one of the few European low-cost airlines that allows companion dogs in both the cabin and the hold. Its main strengths include pets accepted in cabin and hold, competitive pet transport fees, a strong Scandinavian network, a straightforward booking process, and extensive leisure destinations. For travellers flying within Northern Europe, Norwegian is often one of the easiest and most affordable choices.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_norwegian | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel in the passenger cabin inside an approved carrier placed beneath the seat. Cabin transport depends on a maximum combined weight, carrier dimensions, and limited pet capacity. Pets must remain inside the carrier throughout the flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_norwegian | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding cabin limits may travel inside the aircraft's ventilated and temperature-controlled hold. The kennel must comply with **IATA Live Animals Regulations** and allow the dog to stand, turn around and lie comfortably. Acceptance depends on aircraft type and operational conditions.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_norwegian | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds and breeds presenting increased transport risks may be subject to operational restrictions. Always verify eligibility before booking.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify destination-country import rules;
- ensure your dog's vaccinations are up to date;
- choose an IATA-compliant carrier or kennel;
- reserve your dog's place with Norwegian.

Only a limited number of pets are accepted on each flight.

# Connecting flights

Most Norwegian flights operate as point-to-point services. If your itinerary includes another airline, verify each carrier's pet policy and ensure your dog's transport has been confirmed on every flight.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before reserving your dog

Pet capacity is limited on every aircraft.

## Using a non-compliant kennel

Hold transport requires an IATA-compliant kennel.

## Forgetting destination-country regulations

Airline approval never replaces veterinary or customs requirements.

## Assuming every low-cost airline accepts dogs

Most do not. Norwegian is one of the few exceptions.

# Expert advice

Norwegian is one of the best low-cost airlines for travelling with a dog in Northern Europe. Its combination of affordable fares, cabin and hold transport and extensive Scandinavian network makes it an excellent choice for holidays and regional travel.
