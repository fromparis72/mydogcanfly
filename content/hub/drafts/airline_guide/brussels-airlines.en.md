# Flying with a Dog on Brussels Airlines

## Overview

Brussels Airlines is Belgium's national carrier and a member of the Lufthansa Group and Star Alliance. Operating from its hub at **Brussels Airport (BRU)**, it serves destinations throughout Europe, North America and, in particular, an extensive network across Africa.

For travellers flying with a dog, Brussels Airlines follows a policy very similar to the other airlines within the Lufthansa Group. Depending on your dog's size and destination, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Certain oversized animals or special shipments may be transported through Lufthansa Cargo.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Brussels Airlines' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, routing, weather and available pet capacity.

MyDogCanFly evaluates all three before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Brussels Airlines a good choice?

Yes. Brussels Airlines offers a well-established pet programme supported by the operational standards of the Lufthansa Group. Its strengths include pets accepted in both cabin and hold, an excellent European network, one of Europe's strongest networks to Africa, an efficient hub in Brussels, and harmonised procedures with Lufthansa Group airlines. If your destination is in Central Africa, Brussels Airlines is often one of the easiest and most practical options.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_brussels_airlines | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs and cats may travel inside the passenger cabin in an approved carrier placed beneath the seat. Transport is subject to maximum weight limits and the number of pets permitted on each flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_brussels_airlines | hold_policy}}

Larger dogs travel inside a temperature-controlled and pressurised hold in an IATA-compliant kennel. Oversized animals or special transport requests may require Lufthansa Cargo services.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_brussels_airlines | breed_restrictions}}

Additional operational restrictions may apply to brachycephalic breeds and certain powerful breeds.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- review destination-country import requirements;
- measure your carrier or kennel carefully;
- request pet approval from Brussels Airlines.

Because pet capacity is limited, reservations should be completed as early as possible.

# Brussels connections

Brussels Airport is known for efficient transfers. Whenever possible, remain on Brussels Airlines-operated flights, verify pet acceptance on codeshare services, and avoid very short connections when your dog travels in the hold.

# Common mistakes

- Booking before reserving pet space.
- Using a kennel that does not comply with IATA standards.
- Forgetting destination-country veterinary requirements.
- Assuming all Star Alliance airlines follow the same pet policy.

# Expert advice

Brussels Airlines is an excellent choice for travellers flying with a dog, particularly to and from Africa. Keeping your itinerary within the Lufthansa Group generally simplifies transfers, documentation and operational procedures.
