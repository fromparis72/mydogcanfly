# Flying with a Dog on Iberia Express
## Overview
Iberia Express is the low-cost airline of the Iberia Group. Operating primarily from **Madrid–Barajas Airport (MAD)**, it serves an extensive network across Spain, Europe and selected destinations in North Africa. Although part of the Iberia family, Iberia Express has its own operational procedures for transporting pets.
The airline accepts companion animals on many routes. Depending on your dog's size and your itinerary, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Animals that cannot travel under these conditions may, in certain cases, be transported through the IAG Cargo network. Brachycephalic breeds are not accepted in the hold.
Travelling successfully with a dog always depends on three factors:
- Iberia Express' transport policy;
- veterinary import requirements of the destination country;
- operational restrictions related to aircraft type and routing.

# Is Iberia Express a good choice?
Yes.
Iberia Express is an excellent choice for travellers flying with a dog on short and medium-haul routes within Spain and Europe. Thanks to its integration within the Iberia Group, it benefits from well-established pet transport procedures while offering an extensive European network.
Some operational restrictions nevertheless apply depending on the aircraft, destination and the breed of your dog. Brachycephalic breeds are not accepted in the hold.
---
# Cabin, Hold or Cargo
## Cabin
{{fait: airline_iberia_express | cabin_policy}}
Dogs, cats, birds (excluding birds of prey), fish and aquarium turtles may travel in the cabin after prior approval. The combined weight of the pet and carrier must not exceed **8 kg**, and the carrier must respect the airline's maximum dimensions of **45 × 35 × 25 cm**.
---
## Hold
{{fait: airline_iberia_express | hold_policy}}
Dogs and cats exceeding cabin limits travel inside a ventilated, pressurised and climate-controlled hold in an IATA-compliant kennel.
Important conditions include:
- maximum combined weight (pet + kennel): **45 kg**;
- reservation required at least **48 hours before departure**;
- brachycephalic breeds are not accepted in the hold;
- only **two pets per flight** are accepted in the hold on short and medium-haul routes.
---
## Cargo
{{fait: airline_iberia_express | cargo_policy}}
Animals that cannot travel as accompanied baggage may, depending on the itinerary, be transported through **IAG Cargo**.
---
## Assistance Dogs
{{fait: airline_iberia_express | assistance_policy}}
Recognised service dogs travel free of charge and may accompany their handler in the cabin, subject to the airline's applicable conditions.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify your destination country's veterinary requirements;
- request pet approval as early as possible;
- prepare all veterinary documentation;
- ensure your kennel complies with IATA standards;
- confirm any breed or destination-specific restrictions.
---
# Common Mistakes
## Booking too late
Pet transport requests should generally be submitted at least **48 hours before departure**, and pet capacity is limited.
---
## Travelling with a brachycephalic dog in the hold
Brachycephalic dogs and cats are not accepted in the hold for safety reasons.
---
## Using a non-compliant kennel
Only IATA-approved kennels are accepted for hold transport.
---
## Forgetting destination-country regulations
Airline approval never replaces veterinary, customs or governmental import requirements.
---
# Expert Advice
Iberia Express is particularly well suited for travellers flying within Europe with a dog. If your itinerary includes connections with Iberia or another IAG airline, verify that the pet policy remains compatible across every flight segment before confirming your booking.
