# Flying with a Dog on Royal Air Maroc
## Overview
Royal Air Maroc is the national airline of Morocco and a member of the **oneworld** alliance. From its main hub at **Casablanca Mohammed V International Airport (CMN)**, the airline connects Morocco with Europe, Africa, North America, the Middle East and selected destinations in South America.
Royal Air Maroc accepts companion animals on many domestic and international routes. Depending on your dog's size and the applicable regulations, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin**, **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**, or **as cargo** when operationally required.
As with every international journey, successful pet travel depends on three independent factors:
- Royal Air Maroc's transport policy;
- the veterinary regulations of the destination country;
- operational constraints related to aircraft type and itinerary.
MyDogCanFly analyses all three before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Royal Air Maroc a good choice?
Yes.
Royal Air Maroc is one of the best options for travellers flying between Europe and North or West Africa with a dog. Its extensive African network and direct services from Casablanca make it particularly attractive for international itineraries.
Always verify both airline requirements and destination-country veterinary regulations before travelling.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify import requirements for your destination;
- reserve your dog's transport as early as possible;
- prepare all veterinary documentation;
- ensure your kennel complies with IATA standards;
- confirm pet acceptance on every flight segment.
---
# Common Mistakes
## Booking pet transport too late
Each flight accepts only a limited number of animals.
---
## Assuming every destination has identical import rules
Veterinary requirements vary considerably.
---
## Using a kennel that does not comply with IATA standards
Only approved kennels are accepted.
---
## Forgetting transit-country regulations
Transit airports may impose additional requirements.

# Expert Advice
Royal Air Maroc is particularly attractive for travellers flying between Europe and Africa.
