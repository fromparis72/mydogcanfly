# Flying with a Dog on ITA Airways

## Overview

ITA Airways is Italy's national airline and a member of the Lufthansa Group. Operating primarily from Rome Fiumicino and Milan Linate, it connects Italy with Europe, North America, South America, Africa and the Middle East.

For travellers flying with a dog, ITA Airways has become one of Europe's most innovative airlines. In addition to its standard pet policy, the airline has recently introduced **"Large Pet Friendly"** services on selected domestic routes, allowing larger dogs to travel in the cabin under specific conditions.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- ITA Airways' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

Yes. ITA Airways combines a modern fleet with a growing international network and has become one of Europe's most pet-friendly airlines thanks to recent innovations. # Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- confirm pet availability;
- verify your dog's travel weight;
- measure the carrier or crate;
- review destination-country regulations.

ITA recommends requesting pet transport in advance because the number of animals accepted on each flight is limited.

# Rome connections

Rome Fiumicino is ITA Airways' primary hub and offers efficient international connections. If your itinerary includes flights operated by another airline, verify that every carrier accepts pets under compatible conditions.

# Common mistakes

- Booking before reserving the pet.
- Assuming the new "Large Pet Friendly" service applies to every route.
- Ignoring destination-country import requirements.
- Choosing a carrier that exceeds the permitted dimensions.

# Expert advice

ITA Airways has become one of Europe's most interesting airlines for pet owners. If your itinerary is operated entirely by ITA Airways, you will generally benefit from a simpler booking process and more consistent pet procedures.
