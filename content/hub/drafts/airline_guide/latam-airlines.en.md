# Flying with a Dog on LATAM Airlines
## Overview
LATAM Airlines is the largest airline group in Latin America, serving destinations across **South America, North America, Central America, Europe, Oceania and selected routes to Africa**. With major hubs in **Santiago (SCL)**, **São Paulo (GRU)**, **Lima (LIM)**, **Bogotá (BOG)** and **Quito (UIO)**, LATAM offers one of the continent's most comprehensive route networks.
LATAM accepts pets on many domestic and international routes. Depending on your dog's size, destination and operational restrictions, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin**, **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**, or **as cargo** on routes where this is required.
Travelling successfully with a dog always depends on three factors:
- LATAM's transport policy;
- veterinary import requirements of the destination country;
- operational restrictions related to aircraft type and routing.
MyDogCanFly analyses all three before recommending an itinerary.

# Is LATAM a good choice?
Yes.
LATAM is one of the strongest airlines in the world for travelling with a dog throughout South America. Its extensive domestic coverage in Chile, Peru, Brazil, Colombia and Ecuador, combined with a large international network, makes it an excellent choice for both regional and intercontinental journeys.
As with every international itinerary, always verify both LATAM's pet policy and the veterinary requirements of your destination country.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify destination-country veterinary regulations;
- reserve pet transport as early as possible;
- prepare all required veterinary certificates;
- ensure your kennel complies with IATA standards;
- confirm pet acceptance for every flight segment.
---
# Common Mistakes
## Booking too late
Pet capacity is limited on every flight.
---
## Ignoring country-specific import rules
Requirements differ significantly between South American countries.
---
## Using a non-compliant kennel
Only IATA-approved kennels are accepted.
---
## Forgetting codeshare restrictions
Some LATAM itineraries are operated by partner airlines with different pet policies.
---
# Expert Advice
LATAM is one of the best airlines for exploring South America with a dog.
When your itinerary includes more than one country, prepare your veterinary documents for each border crossing—not only your final destination. This is especially important for multi-country trips across the continent.
