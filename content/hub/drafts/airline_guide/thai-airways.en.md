# Flying with a Dog on Thai Airways

## Overview

Thai Airways is Thailand's national airline and a founding member of the **Star Alliance**. From its main hub at **Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK)**, the airline operates an extensive network across **Asia, Europe, Australia and selected destinations in the Middle East**.

Thai Airways accepts companion dogs on many international flights. Depending on your dog's size and the destination, pets generally travel **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Companion animals are **not normally accepted in the passenger cabin**, except for recognised assistance dogs.

As with every international journey, travelling with a dog depends both on the airline's transport policy and on the veterinary regulations of the destination country.

# Is Thai Airways a good choice?

Yes. Thai Airways has extensive experience transporting companion animals on international routes. Its main advantages include an extensive Asian network, long-haul destinations, specialised live-animal handling, a modern fleet, and strong Star Alliance connectivity. For travellers flying to or from Thailand, Thai Airways is one of the leading choices.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_thai_airways | cabin_policy}}

Companion dogs are generally **not accepted in the passenger cabin**. Only recognised assistance dogs travelling with eligible passengers may remain in the cabin, subject to applicable regulations.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_thai_airways | hold_policy}}

Most companion dogs travel inside the aircraft's ventilated, pressurised and temperature-controlled hold. Requirements generally include advance reservation; an IATA-compliant rigid kennel; valid veterinary documentation; and operational acceptance depending on aircraft type and destination. For oversized animals or specific destinations, cargo transport may be required.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_thai_airways | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds may be subject to additional restrictions because of increased respiratory risks. Temporary embargoes may also apply during periods of extreme heat.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify destination-country import requirements;
- ensure your dog's vaccinations are up to date;
- reserve pet transport with Thai Airways;
- prepare an IATA-compliant kennel if travelling in the hold.

Some destinations require import permits, veterinary certificates or quarantine procedures.

# Common mistakes

## Assuming pets may travel in the cabin

Most companion dogs travel in the hold.

## Using a kennel that does not comply with IATA standards

Only compliant kennels are accepted.

## Forgetting destination-country regulations

Airline approval never replaces veterinary or customs requirements.

## Waiting too long before booking

Pet capacity is limited on every flight.

# Expert advice

Thai Airways is an excellent choice for travelling with a dog throughout Southeast Asia and on long-haul routes to Europe. Reserve your dog's transport early and verify all veterinary documentation well before departure, especially when travelling internationally.
