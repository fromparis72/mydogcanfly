# Flying with a Dog on Volotea

## Overview

Volotea is a Spanish low-cost airline specialising in direct flights between medium-sized European cities. From its operational bases in **France, Spain, Italy and Greece**, it serves more than one hundred destinations across Europe.

Unlike many European low-cost airlines, **Volotea allows small dogs and cats to travel in the passenger cabin**. However, **Volotea does not transport companion animals in the aircraft hold**. If your dog exceeds the cabin limits, you will need to choose another airline.

A successful journey always depends on three independent factors:

- Volotea's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as route, aircraft and available pet capacity.

# Is Volotea a good choice?

Yes—provided your dog qualifies for cabin travel. Volotea is one of the few European low-cost airlines that welcomes companion dogs on board. Its main advantages include small dogs accepted in the cabin, competitive pet transport fees, numerous direct routes across Southern Europe, and simple online booking. For owners of larger dogs, however, another airline will be required because Volotea does not offer hold transport.

# Cabin or hold?

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_volotea | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel inside the passenger cabin in an approved **soft-sided carrier** placed beneath the seat. Current requirements include a maximum combined weight of **10 kg** (pet + carrier); maximum carrier dimensions of **50 × 40 × 20 cm**; one pet per passenger; and a maximum of five pets per flight. Pets must remain inside their carrier throughout the journey.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_volotea | hold_policy}}

Volotea **does not transport companion animals in the aircraft hold**. Travellers with medium or large dogs should select another airline offering checked pet transport.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_volotea | breed_restrictions}}

Pets must be healthy, at least **8 weeks old**, and possess all documents required by the destination country. Animals presenting aggressive behaviour or poor health may be refused carriage.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify destination-country regulations;
- reserve your pet during booking;
- prepare an approved soft-sided carrier;
- ensure your pet passport and health documents are valid.

Please note that pets **cannot travel on Volotea flights to or from the United Kingdom, Ireland or Malta**, due to national regulations.

# Common mistakes

## Assuming larger dogs can travel in the hold

They cannot. Volotea only transports pets in the passenger cabin.

## Exceeding the 10 kg limit

The combined weight of the pet, carrier and accessories must not exceed **10 kg**.

## Forgetting route restrictions

Pets are not accepted on flights to or from the United Kingdom, Ireland and Malta.

## Waiting too long before booking

Only **five pets** are accepted on each flight.

# Expert advice

Volotea is an excellent choice for owners of **small dogs** travelling within Southern Europe. If your dog exceeds the cabin limits, choose airlines such as Air France, KLM, Lufthansa or Transavia, which also provide checked hold transport.
