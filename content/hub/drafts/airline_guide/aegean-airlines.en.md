# Flying with a Dog on Aegean Airlines

## Overview

Aegean Airlines is Greece's largest airline and a member of **Star Alliance**. Operating primarily from **Athens International Airport (ATH)** and **Thessaloniki Airport (SKG)**, Aegean serves more than 160 destinations across Greece, Europe, the Middle East and selected destinations in North Africa.

For travellers flying with a dog, Aegean is widely recognised as one of Europe's most pet-friendly airlines. Depending on your dog's size and destination, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Animals exceeding the airline's operational limits may require specialised cargo transport according to local regulations.

A successful journey always depends on three independent factors:

- Aegean Airlines' pet transport policy;
- veterinary and customs regulations of the destination country;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

# Is Aegean Airlines a good choice?

Yes. Aegean is consistently ranked among Europe's best regional airlines and has developed a clear and well-structured pet policy. Its main strengths include pets accepted in both cabin and hold, an extensive domestic Greek network, a strong European route network, a modern Airbus fleet, and competitive pet transport fees. It is particularly well suited for travellers flying to the Greek islands or Southern Europe.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_aegean | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel inside the passenger cabin in an airline-approved carrier placed beneath the seat. Cabin transport depends on the maximum combined weight, carrier dimensions, and available pet capacity. Pets must remain inside their carrier throughout the flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_aegean | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding cabin limits may travel inside the aircraft's ventilated and temperature-controlled hold. The kennel must comply with **IATA Live Animals Regulations** and provide sufficient room for the dog to stand, turn around and lie comfortably. Some aircraft types may have additional size limitations.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_aegean | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds and some powerful breeds may be subject to operational restrictions. Always verify eligibility before purchasing your ticket.

# Before booking

Before confirming your reservation:

- verify destination-country import rules;
- ensure your dog is microchipped and vaccinated;
- select an IATA-compliant carrier or kennel;
- reserve your dog's place with Aegean.

Pet acceptance depends on aircraft type and available capacity. During the summer season, pet allocations often fill quickly.

# Connecting through Athens

Athens International Airport is Aegean's primary hub. For the smoothest experience, remain on Aegean-operated flights whenever possible, verify pet acceptance on partner airlines, and avoid short connections if your dog travels in the hold. Keeping the itinerary entirely on Aegean generally simplifies transfers.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before reserving pet space

Only a limited number of pets are accepted on each aircraft.

## Using a non-compliant kennel

Hold transport requires an IATA-compliant kennel.

## Forgetting destination-country regulations

Airline approval never replaces veterinary or customs requirements.

## Booking partner flights without checking

Codeshare airlines may apply completely different pet policies.

# Expert advice

Aegean is one of the easiest airlines for travelling with a dog within Southern Europe. Its extensive domestic Greek network, modern fleet and straightforward procedures make it particularly attractive for travellers heading to the Greek islands or connecting through Athens. For the easiest journey, reserve your pet immediately after booking your ticket and keep your itinerary entirely on Aegean-operated flights whenever possible.
