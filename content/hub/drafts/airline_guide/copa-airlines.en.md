# Flying with a Dog on Copa Airlines
## Overview
Copa Airlines is the national airline of Panama and a member of the **Star Alliance**. Operating from its hub at **Tocumen International Airport (PTY)**, Copa has built one of the most extensive networks in Latin America, connecting more than 30 countries across North, Central and South America, as well as the Caribbean.
Copa Airlines accepts pets on many domestic and international flights. Depending on your dog's size, destination and operational restrictions, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin**, **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**, or **as cargo** when required.
Travelling successfully with a dog always depends on three factors:
- Copa Airlines' transport policy;
- veterinary import requirements of the destination country;
- operational restrictions related to aircraft type and routing.
MyDogCanFly analyses all three before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Copa Airlines a good choice?
Yes.
Copa Airlines is one of the strongest choices for travellers flying throughout the Americas with a dog. Thanks to its highly efficient Panama City hub, it offers convenient one-stop connections between North America, Central America, South America and the Caribbean.
Before travelling, always verify both Copa Airlines' pet policy and the veterinary import requirements of your destination country.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify veterinary import requirements for your destination;
- reserve pet transport as early as possible;
- prepare all required health documentation;
- ensure your kennel complies with IATA standards;
- confirm pet acceptance on every flight segment.
---
# Common Mistakes
## Booking pet transport too late
Pet capacity is limited on every flight.
---
## Assuming transit through Panama requires no preparation
Some itineraries may involve additional operational or veterinary requirements.
---
## Using a non-compliant kennel
Only IATA-approved kennels are accepted.
---
## Forgetting destination-country regulations
Airline approval never replaces official import requirements.

# Expert Advice
Copa Airlines is particularly attractive for travellers connecting across the Americas.
