# Flying with a Dog on Tunisair
## Overview
Tunisair is the national airline of Tunisia. From its primary hub at **Tunis–Carthage International Airport (TUN)**, the airline serves destinations throughout **Europe, North Africa, the Middle East and West Africa**.
Tunisair accepts companion animals on many scheduled flights. Depending on your dog's size, destination and operational constraints, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin**, **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**, or **as cargo** when required.
A successful international journey always depends on three elements:
- Tunisair's transport policy;
- veterinary import requirements of the destination country;
- operational restrictions related to the aircraft and itinerary.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify destination-country veterinary regulations;
- reserve pet transport as early as possible;
- prepare all required health certificates;
- ensure your kennel complies with IATA standards;
- confirm pet acceptance on every flight.
