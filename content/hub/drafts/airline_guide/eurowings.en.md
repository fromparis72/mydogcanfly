# Flying with a Dog on Eurowings

## Overview

Eurowings is the low-cost airline of the Lufthansa Group. Operating from major bases including **Düsseldorf (DUS), Cologne/Bonn (CGN), Hamburg (HAM), Stuttgart (STR), Prague (PRG), Stockholm (ARN)** and **Palma de Mallorca (PMI)**, it serves hundreds of destinations across Europe and selected routes in North Africa and the Middle East.

Unlike many European low-cost airlines, **Eurowings accepts small dogs in the passenger cabin**. However, **companion animals are not transported in the aircraft hold**. This makes Eurowings an attractive option for owners of small dogs, but unsuitable for travellers with medium or large breeds.

A successful journey depends on three independent factors:

- Eurowings' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs regulations;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, route and available pet capacity.

# Is Eurowings a good choice?

Yes—if your dog qualifies for cabin travel. Eurowings combines affordable fares with one of the most pet-friendly cabin policies among European low-cost airlines. Its main strengths include small dogs accepted in the cabin, an extensive European network, Lufthansa Group operational standards, and straightforward online booking. However, owners of medium and large dogs will generally need another airline because Eurowings does not transport companion animals in the hold.

# Cabin or hold?

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_eurowings | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel inside the passenger cabin in a **soft-sided, leak-proof and ventilated carrier** placed under the seat in front of you. Current requirements include a maximum combined weight of **8 kg** (pet + carrier); maximum carrier dimensions of **55 × 40 × 23 cm**; the dog must be able to stand, turn around and lie down comfortably; and pets must remain inside the carrier throughout the flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_eurowings | hold_policy}}

Eurowings **does not transport companion dogs in the aircraft hold**. Travellers with medium or large dogs should select another airline that offers checked pet transport.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_eurowings | breed_restrictions}}

Category 1 attack dogs (where prohibited by applicable legislation) are not transported. Destination-country regulations may also impose additional restrictions.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify destination-country import requirements;
- reserve your pet during booking;
- prepare a compliant soft-sided carrier;
- complete the required Eurowings declaration form before check-in.

# Common mistakes

## Bringing a hard-sided carrier

Only approved **soft-sided carriers** are accepted in the cabin.

## Assuming large dogs can travel in the hold

They cannot. Eurowings only transports companion animals in the cabin.

## Forgetting destination-country regulations

Airline approval never replaces veterinary or customs requirements.

# Expert advice

Eurowings is one of the best European low-cost airlines for **small dogs**. If your dog weighs more than the cabin limits allow, choose an airline such as Lufthansa, Air France, KLM or Transavia, which also offer checked hold transport.
