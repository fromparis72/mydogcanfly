# Flying with a Dog on easyJet

## Overview

easyJet is one of Europe's largest low-cost airlines, operating an extensive network from bases across the United Kingdom, France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Switzerland and many other European countries.

Unlike most traditional airlines, **easyJet does not transport companion dogs**, either in the passenger cabin or in the aircraft hold. The only exception concerns **recognised assistance dogs** travelling with eligible passengers on approved routes and subject to local regulations.

For travellers wishing to fly with a pet dog, another airline will generally be required.

A successful trip therefore depends on three independent factors:

- easyJet's pet transport policy;
- destination-country regulations;
- identifying an alternative airline if travelling with a companion dog.

# Is easyJet a good choice?

For most dog owners, **no**. easyJet's business model does not include transporting companion animals. If you are travelling with a pet dog, MyDogCanFly will generally recommend another airline serving the same destination. However, easyJet remains an excellent option for passengers travelling with an officially recognised assistance dog.

# Companion dogs

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_easyjet | cabin_policy}}

Companion dogs are **not accepted in the cabin**.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_easyjet | hold_policy}}

easyJet does **not transport dogs in the aircraft hold**.

Assistance dogs: recognised assistance dogs may travel free of charge on eligible routes, subject to advance approval and documentation requirements. Additional rules apply depending on the destination country, particularly for flights involving the United Kingdom.

# Before booking

Before purchasing an easyJet ticket:

- verify whether your dog is an officially recognised assistance dog;
- check destination-country regulations;
- if travelling with a companion dog, choose another airline.

Booking first and searching for pet transport afterwards usually leads to disappointment.

# Common mistakes

## Assuming all airlines accept pets

Many low-cost airlines—including easyJet—do not.

## Booking before checking the pet policy

Companion dogs cannot be added later.

## Confusing emotional support animals with assistance dogs

Emotional support animals are not recognised as assistance dogs on easyJet.
