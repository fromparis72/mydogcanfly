# Flying with a Dog on Virgin Atlantic

## Overview

Virgin Atlantic is one of the United Kingdom's leading long-haul airlines. Operating primarily from London Heathrow and Manchester, it serves North America, the Caribbean, Africa, the Middle East and selected destinations in Asia.

For travellers flying with a dog, Virgin Atlantic follows a model that differs from many European airlines. On most international routes, privately owned pets are transported through approved specialist pet shipping services rather than as accompanied baggage.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Virgin Atlantic's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, routing, weather and cargo availability.

# Is Virgin Atlantic a good choice?

Yes, but only if you understand its transport model. Unlike many airlines, Virgin Atlantic generally does **not** accept privately owned pets as checked baggage. Instead, pets usually travel as manifested cargo through approved animal transport specialists, particularly on routes involving the United Kingdom. Although this requires additional preparation, it also provides a highly structured process designed specifically for international animal transport.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- confirm whether your route requires cargo transport;
- contact an approved pet transport agent if necessary;
- review destination-country regulations;
- verify crate dimensions and travel dates.

Because cargo bookings require more coordination than standard passenger reservations, planning several weeks in advance is strongly recommended.

# Heathrow connections

London Heathrow is one of the world's busiest airports. If your itinerary combines Virgin Atlantic with another airline, verify that the transport method remains compatible throughout the journey. A passenger flight combined with a cargo-only segment may require additional logistics.

# Common mistakes

- Assuming pets travel as checked baggage.
- Waiting too long before contacting a pet transport company.
- Underestimating UK import procedures.
- Choosing a crate that does not meet IATA standards.

# Expert advice

Virgin Atlantic is an excellent airline for long-haul travel, but it is best suited to owners who are comfortable using professional pet relocation services. For many UK itineraries, this is not an optional service—it is the normal transport process.
