# Flying with a Dog on Aeromexico
## Overview
Aeromexico is Mexico's flagship airline and a member of the **SkyTeam** alliance. Operating primarily from **Mexico City International Airport (MEX)**, it serves an extensive network across **Mexico, North America, Central America, South America, Europe and Asia**.
Aeromexico accepts companion animals on many domestic and international flights. Depending on your dog's size, destination and operational requirements, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin**, **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**, or **as cargo** for oversized animals or specific routes.
A successful journey with your dog depends on three separate factors:
- Aeromexico's transport policy;
- veterinary import requirements of the destination country;
- operational limitations related to aircraft type and itinerary.
MyDogCanFly analyses all three before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Aeromexico a good choice?
Yes.
Aeromexico is one of the best options for travelling with a dog to or from Mexico. Its extensive domestic network, numerous international destinations and SkyTeam membership make it particularly suitable for both direct and connecting itineraries.
Always verify both Aeromexico's pet policy and the veterinary requirements of your destination before travelling.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify veterinary import requirements for your destination;
- reserve pet transport as early as possible;
- prepare all required veterinary documentation;
- ensure your kennel complies with IATA standards if your dog travels in the hold;
- confirm pet acceptance on every flight segment.
---
# Common Mistakes
## Booking pet transport too late
Each flight accepts only a limited number of animals.
---
## Assuming transit countries have no requirements
Transit airports may impose additional veterinary conditions.
---
## Using a kennel that does not comply with IATA standards
Only approved kennels are accepted.
---
## Forgetting destination-country regulations
Airline approval never replaces government import requirements.
---
# Expert Advice
Aeromexico is particularly well suited for travellers flying between Mexico, the United States, Canada and Europe.
If your itinerary includes a connection or codeshare flight, verify that every operating carrier accepts pets under the same conditions before departure.
