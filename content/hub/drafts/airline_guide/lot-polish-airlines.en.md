# Flying with a Dog on LOT Polish Airlines

## Overview

LOT Polish Airlines is Poland's national airline and a member of Star Alliance. Operating primarily from Warsaw Chopin Airport, LOT connects Central and Eastern Europe with North America, Asia and the Middle East.

For travellers flying with a dog, LOT offers an attractive combination of competitive fares and a growing long-haul network, making it an interesting option for journeys originating in Central Europe.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- LOT's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

MyDogCanFly evaluates all three together before recommending an itinerary.

# Is LOT Polish Airlines a good choice?

Yes. LOT is particularly attractive for travellers based in Central and Eastern Europe who want to avoid connecting through larger Western European hubs. Its modern long-haul fleet and relatively compact hub in Warsaw often make transfers faster and easier than at some of Europe's busiest airports.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- check your dog's travel weight;
- confirm carrier or crate dimensions;
- review destination-country import requirements.

LOT allows pet reservations online, but availability is limited on every flight.

# Warsaw connections

Warsaw Chopin Airport is relatively easy to navigate and offers efficient connections. If another airline operates part of your itinerary, verify that its pet policy is compatible with LOT's.

# Common mistakes

- Booking the ticket before reserving the pet.
- Choosing a carrier exceeding LOT's cabin dimensions.
- Forgetting destination-country veterinary requirements.
- Assuming all Star Alliance airlines have identical pet policies.

# Expert advice

LOT is one of the best options for travellers departing from Central Europe. Its compact hub and growing long-haul network often make travelling with a dog simpler than connecting through larger European airports.
