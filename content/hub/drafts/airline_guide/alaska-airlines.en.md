# Flying with a Dog on Alaska Airlines

## Overview

Alaska Airlines is one of the largest airlines on the west coast of the United States. Through its hubs in Seattle, Portland, Anchorage, Los Angeles and San Francisco, it serves more than one hundred destinations across the United States, Canada, Mexico, Costa Rica, Belize and selected international markets.

For pet owners, Alaska Airlines has long enjoyed one of the strongest reputations in North America. Its **Pet Connect®** program and decades of experience transporting animals have made it a reference airline for domestic travel with dogs.

A successful trip always depends on three independent elements:

- Alaska Airlines' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and pet availability.

MyDogCanFly evaluates all three before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Alaska Airlines a good choice?

Yes. Alaska Airlines is widely regarded as one of the most pet-friendly airlines in North America. Its strongest advantages include decades of experience transporting pets, an excellent domestic network, the dedicated Pet Connect® service, a strong reputation among veterinarians and breeders, and numerous nonstop flights across the western United States. For travellers flying within North America, Alaska Airlines is often one of the safest and simplest choices.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_alaska | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs and cats may travel in the cabin inside an approved carrier placed beneath the seat in front of the passenger.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_alaska | hold_policy}}

Larger dogs may travel in a temperature-controlled compartment inside an IATA-compliant crate. Certain itineraries may instead use Alaska Air Cargo under the Pet Connect® program.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_alaska | breed_restrictions}}

Always remember that airline approval never replaces destination-country veterinary requirements.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- check your dog's travel weight;
- measure your carrier or crate;
- review destination-country regulations.

Pet reservations should be requested as early as possible because capacity is limited on every flight.

# Seattle connections

Seattle-Tacoma International Airport is Alaska Airlines' primary hub. Whenever possible, choose an itinerary operated entirely by Alaska Airlines. If another airline is involved, verify that its pet policy is compatible with Alaska's requirements.

# Common mistakes

- Booking before reserving the pet.
- Choosing a carrier larger than permitted.
- Ignoring seasonal weather restrictions.
- Assuming partner airlines follow identical pet policies.

# Expert advice

Alaska Airlines is consistently ranked among the most pet-friendly airlines in North America. If your itinerary is available on Alaska Airlines, particularly within the United States or Canada, it will often be one of the easiest options for travelling with your dog.
