# Flying with a Dog on Virgin Australia

## Overview

Virgin Australia is Australia's second-largest airline, operating an extensive domestic network together with selected international routes to New Zealand, the Pacific Islands and other nearby destinations.

Unlike many airlines around the world, Virgin Australia currently **does not allow pets to travel in the passenger cabin** (except accredited assistance dogs). Dogs travel either as checked pets on eligible domestic services or via Virgin Australia Cargo, depending on the route and aircraft.

For dog owners travelling within Australia, Virgin Australia remains one of the country's principal carriers and offers well-established procedures for transporting animals safely.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Virgin Australia's pet transport policy;
- destination-country or state veterinary requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available capacity.

# Is Virgin Australia a good choice?

Yes. Virgin Australia is one of Australia's two major full-service airlines and has extensive experience transporting pets. Its main strengths include excellent domestic coverage, a modern fleet, dedicated animal transport procedures, and reliable connections between Australia's largest cities. If your journey is entirely domestic, Virgin Australia is often an excellent option.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- confirm your dog's travel weight;
- measure your crate carefully;
- review Australian state or international import requirements.

Pet bookings should always be arranged in advance.

# Domestic connections

Australia involves very long flying distances. Whenever possible, choose direct flights to minimise handling and reduce stress for your dog. If another airline operates part of your itinerary, confirm that both carriers accept pets under compatible conditions.

# Common mistakes

- Assuming pets can travel in the passenger cabin.
- Booking before reserving pet transport.
- Choosing a crate that is too small.
- Ignoring Australian biosecurity requirements.

# Expert advice

Virgin Australia is an excellent choice for domestic Australian travel. For long journeys such as Perth–Sydney or Brisbane–Adelaide, choosing a nonstop flight significantly improves your dog's travel experience.
