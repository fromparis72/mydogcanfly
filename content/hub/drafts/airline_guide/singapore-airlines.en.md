# Flying with a Dog on Singapore Airlines

## Overview

Singapore Airlines is one of the world's highest-rated premium airlines. From its award-winning hub at Singapore Changi Airport, it connects Asia with Europe, North America, Australia, New Zealand, the Middle East and Africa.

For travellers flying with a dog, Singapore Airlines applies a policy that differs from many European carriers. **Pets are generally not permitted in the passenger cabin**, except for recognised assistance dogs. Most dogs travel as **checked baggage**, while heavier animals or certain destinations require transport through an approved cargo agent.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Singapore Airlines' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available capacity.

MyDogCanFly evaluates all three before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Singapore Airlines a good choice?

Yes. Singapore Airlines has an excellent reputation for long-haul service and applies strict procedures for transporting animals. Its strengths include one of the world's best hub airports, exceptional operational reliability, experienced live-animal handling, and an extensive intercontinental network. The airline accepts **dogs and cats only** and requires advance approval before travel.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_singapore_airlines | cabin_policy}}

Only recognised assistance dogs may travel inside the passenger cabin. Emotional support dogs are no longer accepted as cabin animals.

Checked baggage / cargo: {{fait: airline_singapore_airlines | hold_policy}}

Dogs normally travel as checked baggage in an IATA-compliant crate. When the combined weight of the dog and crate exceeds **32 kg**, transportation must be arranged through an approved cargo agent. Some destinations—including Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Hong Kong and the United Kingdom—also require cargo transport regardless of weight.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_singapore_airlines | breed_restrictions}}

Singapore Airlines prohibits many brachycephalic breeds, including Bulldogs, Pugs, Boxers, Chow Chows, Shih Tzus and several Mastiff breeds because of the increased health risks associated with air transport.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- contact Singapore Airlines **at least two weeks before departure**;
- verify your dog's breed eligibility;
- confirm crate dimensions;
- prepare all veterinary certificates and import permits.

The airline requests detailed information about your dog before approving transport.

# Singapore Changi connections

Singapore Changi Airport is consistently ranked among the world's best airports. If your itinerary includes another airline, verify that every operating carrier applies compatible pet transport rules. Also remember that certain Singapore Airlines flights and aircraft types (notably some Boeing 737 services) cannot transport pets.

# Common mistakes

- Assuming pets can travel in the passenger cabin.
- Waiting until the last minute to request approval.
- Forgetting destination-specific import restrictions.
- Choosing a brachycephalic breed that is prohibited.

# Expert advice

Singapore Airlines is an excellent choice for international travel with a dog **provided your itinerary satisfies all operational restrictions**. Before booking, always verify aircraft type, destination restrictions, combined dog-and-crate weight, and whether checked baggage or cargo transport applies. This preparation avoids most last-minute surprises.
