# Flying with a Dog on Austrian Airlines

## Overview

Austrian Airlines is Austria's national carrier and a member of the Lufthansa Group and Star Alliance. From its hub at **Vienna International Airport (VIE)**, it connects Europe with North America, Asia, the Middle East and Africa.

For travellers flying with a dog, Austrian Airlines offers one of the most flexible pet policies in Europe. Depending on your dog's size and destination, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin**, **in the climate-controlled hold**, or via **Lufthansa Cargo** for oversized or specialised shipments.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Austrian Airlines' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, routing, weather and available pet capacity.

# Is Austrian Airlines a good choice?

Yes. Austrian Airlines combines the operational standards of the Lufthansa Group with the convenience of Vienna Airport, one of Europe's easiest hubs for connecting flights. Its main advantages include pets accepted both in the cabin and in the hold, excellent connections throughout Central and Eastern Europe, a reliable long-haul network, integration with Lufthansa Group services, and efficient transfer procedures. For many journeys between Western Europe, Eastern Europe and Asia, Austrian Airlines is an excellent option.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_austrian | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs and cats may travel inside the passenger cabin in an approved carrier placed under the seat. Transport is subject to weight limits, carrier dimensions and available capacity.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_austrian | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding cabin limits travel in a temperature-controlled, pressurised hold inside an IATA-compliant kennel. Large animals or special transport requests may be handled through Lufthansa Cargo.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_austrian | breed_restrictions}}

Additional restrictions may apply to brachycephalic breeds and certain powerful breeds.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet eligibility;
- measure your carrier or kennel;
- review destination-country regulations;
- request pet approval from Austrian Airlines.

Reservations should be made as early as possible because only a limited number of pets are accepted on each flight.

# Vienna connections

Vienna Airport is well known for fast and efficient transfers. Whenever possible, remain on Austrian Airlines-operated flights, verify pet acceptance on codeshare services, and avoid very short connections if your dog travels in the hold.

# Common mistakes

- Booking flights before reserving pet space.
- Using a kennel that does not comply with IATA standards.
- Forgetting veterinary import requirements.
- Assuming partner airlines apply Austrian Airlines' pet policy.

# Expert advice

Austrian Airlines is one of Europe's most reliable carriers for travelling with a dog. Its combination of Vienna's efficient hub and the Lufthansa Group's animal transport standards makes it particularly attractive for journeys involving Central Europe, the Balkans or Eastern Europe. Whenever possible, keep the entire itinerary within the Lufthansa Group to simplify transfers.
