# Flying with a Dog on Air Tahiti Nui

## Overview

Air Tahiti Nui is the international airline of **French Polynesia**. From its main hub at **Tahiti Faa'a International Airport (PPT)**, it connects French Polynesia with **Paris, Los Angeles, Seattle, Tokyo and Auckland**.

Air Tahiti Nui accepts companion dogs on selected international flights. Depending on their size and the destination, dogs may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Oversized animals or specific itineraries may require transport as cargo.

Travelling to French Polynesia requires compliance with strict veterinary import regulations in addition to the airline's own transport policy.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_air_tahiti_nui | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel in the passenger cabin when permitted by the route and applicable regulations. Acceptance depends on carrier dimensions, applicable weight limits, available pet capacity, and destination requirements. Pets must remain inside their carrier throughout the flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_air_tahiti_nui | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding cabin limits travel inside the aircraft's ventilated, pressurised and temperature-controlled hold. Requirements include advance reservation; an IATA-compliant rigid kennel; valid veterinary documentation; and operational acceptance. Oversized animals may need to travel as cargo.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_air_tahiti_nui | breed_restrictions}}

Certain breeds, including some brachycephalic dogs, may be subject to operational restrictions. Weather conditions may also affect pet transport.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify French Polynesia's import requirements;
- ensure your dog is microchipped and properly vaccinated;
- reserve pet transport with Air Tahiti Nui;
- prepare an IATA-compliant kennel if required.

French Polynesia applies particularly strict import rules, including veterinary certificates and official approvals before departure.

# Common mistakes

## Starting import procedures too late

French Polynesia requires advance veterinary preparation.

## Assuming airline approval guarantees entry

Airline acceptance never replaces local veterinary authorities' approval.

## Using a non-compliant kennel

Only IATA-approved kennels are accepted.

## Forgetting destination-specific regulations

French Polynesia has stricter animal import rules than many other destinations.

# Expert advice

Air Tahiti Nui is the reference airline for travelling with a dog between mainland France and French Polynesia. Begin preparing your dog's import documentation several months before departure, as the administrative process is often longer than travellers expect.
