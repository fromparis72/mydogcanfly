# Flying with a Dog on Air France

## Overview

Air France is France's national airline and one of Europe's largest international carriers. Operating primarily from **Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG)** and **Paris-Orly (ORY)**, it serves more than 190 destinations across Europe, Africa, North America, South America, Asia, the Caribbean and the Indian Ocean.

For dog owners, Air France is widely regarded as one of the easiest legacy airlines to use. Depending on your dog's size and your destination, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Only exceptionally large animals or routes with local regulatory requirements require cargo transport.

Because Air France belongs to the **Air France–KLM Group** and works closely with SkyTeam partners, it also offers excellent worldwide connectivity for international journeys.

A successful trip always depends on three independent factors:

- Air France's transport policy;
- veterinary and customs requirements of the destination country;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, embargoes and available pet capacity.

# Is Air France a good choice?

Absolutely. Air France has transported companion animals for decades and offers one of Europe's most balanced pet policies. Its main advantages include pets accepted in both cabin and hold, an extensive international network, excellent connectivity via Paris-CDG, climate-controlled animal compartments, and simple online booking followed by pet reservation. It is particularly attractive for travellers departing from France or connecting through Paris.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_air_france | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel inside the passenger cabin in an approved soft-sided carrier placed beneath the seat. Cabin transport is subject to a maximum combined weight, carrier dimensions, and limited pet capacity per flight. For everyone's comfort, pets must remain inside the carrier throughout the flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_air_france | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding cabin limits travel inside the aircraft's ventilated and temperature-controlled hold. The travel crate must comply with **IATA Live Animals Regulations** and allow the dog to stand naturally, turn around, and lie comfortably. The hold is pressurised and specifically designed for live animal transport.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_air_france | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds and some categories of powerful dogs may be subject to additional operational restrictions or may not be accepted depending on destination-country legislation. Always verify eligibility before purchasing your ticket.

# Before booking

Before confirming your reservation:

- verify destination-country import rules;
- ensure your dog's vaccinations are up to date;
- choose an IATA-compliant carrier;
- request pet transport with Air France.

Pet acceptance depends on available space. During busy holiday periods, flights often reach their pet capacity well before passenger capacity.

# Connecting through Paris

Paris Charles de Gaulle is one of Europe's largest international hubs. For the smoothest experience, keep your itinerary entirely on Air France whenever possible, avoid very short connections if your dog travels in the hold, and verify pet acceptance on every codeshare flight. Remaining on Air France-operated flights generally simplifies transfers considerably.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before reserving your dog

Pet capacity is limited. Reserve your dog's place immediately after purchasing your ticket.

## Using a non-compliant travel crate

IATA compliance is mandatory for hold transport. An unsuitable kennel may lead to boarding refusal.

## Forgetting destination-country requirements

Airline approval does not replace import permits, veterinary certificates, microchip regulations or quarantine requirements.

## Booking codeshare flights without checking

Some partner airlines apply completely different pet policies. Always verify every operating carrier.

# Expert advice

Air France remains one of the best airlines in Europe for travelling with a dog. Its combination of flexible cabin transport, reliable climate-controlled hold facilities and an outstanding international network makes it suitable for everything from short European city breaks to complex long-haul journeys. For the easiest experience, reserve your dog's place immediately after booking and keep your itinerary entirely on Air France whenever possible.
