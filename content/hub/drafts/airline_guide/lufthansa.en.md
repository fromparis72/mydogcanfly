# Flying with a Dog on Lufthansa

## Overview

Lufthansa is Germany's flag carrier and one of Europe's largest airlines. Through its major hubs at **Frankfurt (FRA)** and **Munich (MUC)**, it connects more than 200 destinations across Europe, North America, South America, Africa, Asia and the Middle East.

For travellers flying with a dog, Lufthansa is widely regarded as one of the best European airlines. Depending on your dog's size and the regulations of your destination country, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin**, **in the aircraft's climate-controlled cargo hold**, or via **Lufthansa Cargo** for specific situations.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Lufthansa's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

# Is Lufthansa a good choice?

Yes. Lufthansa is one of Europe's benchmark airlines for travelling with pets. Its main strengths include pets accepted both in the cabin and in the hold, an extensive intercontinental network, a modern fleet, dedicated Lufthansa Cargo services for complex animal transport, and highly structured booking procedures.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_lufthansa | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs and cats weighing **up to 8 kg including the carrier** may travel in the passenger cabin inside an approved soft-sided carrier placed under the seat. Each passenger may travel with a maximum of two pets under specific conditions.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_lufthansa | hold_policy}}

Dogs weighing more than 8 kg (including their travel container) generally travel in the aircraft's **air-conditioned cargo hold** inside an IATA-compliant kennel. Large animals that cannot travel as accompanied baggage may be transported via **Lufthansa Cargo** through an approved animal freight forwarder.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_lufthansa | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds and some fighting dog breeds are subject to additional restrictions or may not be accepted in the cargo hold because of welfare considerations.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify your dog's eligibility;
- confirm destination-country import rules;
- measure your carrier or kennel carefully;
- request pet approval from Lufthansa.

Lufthansa recommends registering pets **at least 72 hours before departure**, although some requests may still be accepted up to 24 hours before departure through the Service Center if space remains available. Capacity is limited on every flight.

# Frankfurt and Munich connections

Frankfurt and Munich are among Europe's largest connecting hubs. Whenever possible, keep the itinerary entirely on Lufthansa-operated flights, verify pet acceptance on every codeshare segment, and avoid very short transfer times when your dog travels in the hold.

# Common mistakes

- Booking flights before confirming pet availability.
- Using a kennel that does not meet IATA standards.
- Forgetting destination-country veterinary regulations.
- Assuming partner airlines apply Lufthansa's pet policy.

# Expert advice

Lufthansa is one of the safest choices for international travel with a dog. Its combination of cabin transport, climate-controlled hold transport and Lufthansa Cargo makes it suitable for almost every travel scenario, from a weekend trip within Europe to complex intercontinental relocations. For the smoothest experience, register your dog at the same time you book your own ticket and keep your itinerary entirely within the Lufthansa Group whenever possible.

### Should I reserve early?

Yes. Lufthansa recommends registering pets at least **72 hours before departure**, as the number of animals accepted on each flight is limited.

Always. Lufthansa's approval never replaces veterinary, customs or quarantine requirements imposed by the destination country.
