# Flying with a Dog on Air Mauritius
## Overview
Air Mauritius is the national airline of Mauritius. From its main hub at **Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU)**, it connects Mauritius with **Europe, Africa, Asia, Australia and neighbouring Indian Ocean islands**.
Air Mauritius accepts companion animals on selected international routes. Depending on your dog's size, destination and operational requirements, pets generally travel **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**, while oversized animals or certain destinations may require transport as cargo. Only recognised assistance dogs may be accepted in the passenger cabin where regulations permit.
Travelling with a dog to Mauritius also requires compliance with one of the world's strictest animal import procedures.

Yes.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify Mauritius import regulations;
- apply for import permits if required;
- prepare all veterinary certificates;
- reserve pet transport well in advance;
- use an IATA-compliant kennel.

# Expert Advice
Air Mauritius offers reliable pet transport, but Mauritius is one of the most regulated destinations in the Indian Ocean.
Begin preparing your dog's file several months before departure and never assume that airline approval alone is sufficient.
