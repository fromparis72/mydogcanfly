# Flying with a Dog on American Airlines

## Overview

American Airlines is one of the world's largest airlines and a founding member of the **oneworld Alliance**. From its major hubs at **Dallas/Fort Worth (DFW), Charlotte (CLT), Chicago O'Hare (ORD), Miami (MIA), Philadelphia (PHL), Phoenix (PHX), Washington National (DCA), New York (JFK)** and **Los Angeles (LAX)**, the airline connects hundreds of destinations throughout North America, Latin America, Europe and Asia.

For travellers flying with a dog, American Airlines offers **in-cabin transport for eligible small dogs** on most domestic flights and many international routes. Unlike several European airlines, accompanied checked-pet transport has been significantly reduced and is now available only in limited circumstances. Large dogs generally require specialised pet transport services or cargo solutions depending on the route and current regulations.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- American Airlines' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, routing, weather and available pet capacity.

# Is American Airlines a good choice?

Yes, particularly for owners of small dogs. Its main strengths include one of the world's largest route networks, excellent domestic U.S. coverage, a strong Latin American network, pets accepted in the passenger cabin, and a straightforward online booking process. Owners of larger dogs should carefully review current transport options before booking, as accompanied hold transport is no longer broadly available.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_american | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel inside the passenger cabin in an airline-approved carrier that fits beneath the seat. Each passenger may bring one pet carrier, and space is limited on every flight. Pets must remain inside the carrier throughout boarding and the flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_american | hold_policy}}

Checked-pet transport is now available only on selected itineraries and under specific operational conditions. Many larger dogs require specialised cargo or professional pet relocation services depending on destination-country regulations and airline policy. Operational embargoes may also apply during periods of extreme heat or cold.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_american | breed_restrictions}}

Additional restrictions may apply to brachycephalic breeds, large kennels and certain destinations. Always verify eligibility before purchasing your ticket.

# Before booking

Before confirming your reservation:

- verify destination-country import requirements;
- ensure your dog is properly identified and vaccinated;
- reserve your dog's place immediately;
- verify kennel dimensions if hold transport applies.

International travel may require health certificates, import permits or additional veterinary documentation.

# Connecting through the United States

American Airlines operates some of North America's busiest airports. Whenever possible, remain on American-operated flights, verify every codeshare segment, and allow sufficient transfer time. Partner airlines may apply different pet policies.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before reserving cabin space

Pet capacity is limited on every flight.

## Assuming all international routes accept pets

Certain destinations prohibit in-cabin pets or require cargo transport.

## Forgetting destination-country regulations

Airline approval never replaces veterinary or customs requirements.

## Ignoring seasonal embargoes

Very high or very low temperatures may affect pet acceptance.

# Expert advice

American Airlines is an excellent choice for travelling with a **small dog** within North America or to many international destinations. If your dog exceeds cabin limits, verify transport options before purchasing your ticket, as they vary considerably depending on the route, aircraft and destination country.
