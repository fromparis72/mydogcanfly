# Flying with a Dog on Philippine Airlines

## Overview

Philippine Airlines (PAL) is the national airline of the Philippines and one of Asia's oldest carriers. From its main hubs at **Manila (MNL)**, **Cebu (CEB)** and **Davao (DVO)**, the airline serves an extensive network across **Asia, North America, Australia and the Middle East**.

Philippine Airlines accepts companion animals on selected flights. Depending on the route and operational conditions, dogs generally travel **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Companion animals are **not normally accepted in the passenger cabin**, except for recognised assistance dogs.

International travel with a dog depends on both Philippine Airlines' transport policy and the veterinary import regulations of the destination country.

# Is Philippine Airlines a good choice?

Yes. Philippine Airlines has extensive experience transporting companion animals on domestic and international services. Its main advantages include an extensive domestic Philippine network, long-haul international destinations, dedicated live-animal handling, a modern long-haul fleet, and strong regional connectivity. For travellers flying to or from the Philippines, it is one of the most practical choices.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_philippine_airlines | cabin_policy}}

Companion dogs are generally **not accepted in the passenger cabin**. Only recognised assistance dogs travelling with eligible passengers may remain in the cabin, subject to applicable regulations.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_philippine_airlines | hold_policy}}

Most companion dogs travel inside the aircraft's ventilated, pressurised and temperature-controlled hold. Requirements generally include advance reservation; an IATA-compliant rigid kennel; valid veterinary documentation; and acceptance depending on aircraft type and destination. Some routes or oversized animals may require transport as cargo.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_philippine_airlines | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds may be subject to operational restrictions because of increased respiratory risks. Seasonal weather restrictions may also apply.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify destination-country import requirements;
- ensure your dog's vaccinations are current;
- reserve pet transport with Philippine Airlines;
- prepare an IATA-compliant kennel.

Some destinations require import permits, veterinary certificates or quarantine procedures.

# Common mistakes

## Assuming pets may travel in the cabin

Most companion animals travel in the hold.

## Forgetting import regulations

Airline approval never replaces veterinary or customs requirements.

## Using a kennel that does not comply with IATA standards

Only compliant kennels are accepted.

## Waiting too long before booking

Pet capacity is limited on every flight.

# Expert advice

Philippine Airlines is a reliable option for travelling with a dog to or from the Philippines. For international journeys, confirm both airline requirements and destination-country regulations several weeks before departure.
