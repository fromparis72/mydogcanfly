# Flying with a Dog on ANA (All Nippon Airways)

## Overview

All Nippon Airways (ANA) is Japan's largest airline and a member of the **Star Alliance**. From its hubs at **Tokyo Haneda (HND), Tokyo Narita (NRT), Osaka Itami (ITM)** and **Osaka Kansai (KIX)**, ANA serves an extensive domestic network as well as destinations throughout Asia, Europe, North America and Oceania.

For companion dogs, ANA has a distinctive policy. **Pets are generally transported in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold and are not accepted in the passenger cabin**, except for recognised assistance dogs.

Travelling with a dog to Japan also requires compliance with one of the world's strictest animal import procedures, including microchipping, rabies vaccinations, antibody testing and advance notification to the Japanese authorities. These procedures are entirely separate from the airline's acceptance of your pet.

MyDogCanFly analyses both the airline's policy and Japan's import rules before recommending an itinerary.

# Is ANA a good choice?

Yes. ANA is one of Asia's highest-rated airlines and has extensive experience transporting companion animals safely. Its main strengths include an extensive domestic Japanese network, a large international network, specialised pet handling procedures, excellent operational reliability, and a modern fleet. However, owners should remember that **Japan's import regulations are often more demanding than the airline's own requirements**.

# Cabin, hold or assistance dogs

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_ana | cabin_policy}}

Companion dogs are **not accepted in the passenger cabin**. Only recognised assistance dogs travelling with eligible passengers may remain in the cabin in accordance with Japanese regulations.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_ana | hold_policy}}

Companion dogs normally travel inside the aircraft's ventilated and temperature-controlled hold. Requirements include an IATA-compliant rigid kennel; advance reservation; operational acceptance based on aircraft type; and health and vaccination documentation. Certain weather conditions may temporarily restrict pet transport.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_ana | breed_restrictions}}

ANA may restrict transport of certain brachycephalic breeds because of increased respiratory risks. Additional restrictions may apply depending on season and destination.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify Japan's import regulations;
- ensure your dog has an ISO-compatible microchip;
- complete the required rabies vaccination schedule;
- arrange the rabies antibody blood test when required;
- notify Japanese Animal Quarantine Services within the required timeframe;
- reserve your dog's transport with ANA.

Failure to complete Japan's import procedure may result in quarantine or refusal of entry, even if ANA accepts your booking.

# Common mistakes

## Starting Japan's import process too late

Japan's import procedure may require several months of preparation.

## Assuming airline approval is sufficient

ANA's acceptance does not replace Japanese quarantine requirements.

## Using a non-compliant kennel

Only kennels meeting IATA standards are accepted for hold transport.

## Ignoring seasonal restrictions

Extreme temperatures may affect the transport of certain breeds.

# Expert advice

ANA is one of the best airlines for travelling with a dog to Japan. The real challenge is rarely the flight itself—it is preparing Japan's import documentation correctly. Start planning several months before departure to avoid unnecessary delays or quarantine.
