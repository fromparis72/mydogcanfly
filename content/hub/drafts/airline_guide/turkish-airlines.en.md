# Flying with a Dog on Turkish Airlines

## Overview

Turkish Airlines is the flag carrier of Türkiye and currently serves **more countries than any other airline in the world**. From its global hub at **Istanbul Airport (IST)**, the airline connects Europe, Asia, Africa, the Middle East, North America, South America and Oceania.

For travellers flying with a dog, Turkish Airlines is one of the most versatile international carriers. Depending on your dog's size, breed and destination, pets may travel **in the cabin** or **in the aircraft hold**, making it an attractive option for both short- and long-haul travel.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Turkish Airlines' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, routing, weather and available pet capacity.

MyDogCanFly evaluates all three before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Turkish Airlines a good choice?

Yes. Turkish Airlines is one of the world's best airlines for travelling internationally with a dog. Its major strengths include the largest international destination network in the world, pets accepted both in the cabin and in the hold, a modern long-haul fleet, excellent global connectivity through Istanbul, and competitive fares on many long-haul routes. For many intercontinental journeys, Turkish Airlines offers one of the widest ranges of routing options available.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_turkish | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs and cats may travel inside the passenger cabin in an approved soft-sided carrier placed beneath the seat. Cabin travel is subject to maximum weight and size limitations, and approval must be obtained before departure.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_turkish | hold_policy}}

Larger dogs travel inside a ventilated, pressurised hold in an IATA-compliant travel kennel. Certain aircraft or destinations may require additional operational checks before approval.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_turkish | breed_restrictions}}

Brachycephalic breeds and certain powerful dog breeds may be subject to additional restrictions or seasonal limitations.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- confirm your dog's total travel weight;
- measure your carrier or kennel;
- review destination-country import regulations;
- request pet approval from Turkish Airlines.

Pet acceptance is capacity-controlled, so early booking is strongly recommended.

# Istanbul connections

Istanbul Airport is one of the world's largest connecting hubs. If your itinerary includes another airline, verify that both carriers accept pets under compatible rules. Whenever possible, keep your itinerary entirely on Turkish Airlines to simplify the journey.

# Common mistakes

- Booking flights before reserving the pet.
- Choosing a kennel that does not comply with IATA standards.
- Forgetting destination-country veterinary regulations.
- Assuming every codeshare flight accepts pets.

# Expert advice

Turkish Airlines combines one of the world's largest route networks with a relatively flexible pet policy. If your destination is served directly through Istanbul, keeping the entire itinerary on Turkish Airlines usually offers the simplest travel experience for both you and your dog.
