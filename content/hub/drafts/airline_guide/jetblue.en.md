# Flying with a Dog on JetBlue

## Overview

JetBlue is one of the largest airlines in the United States, serving an extensive network throughout North America, the Caribbean, Central America and selected destinations in Europe.

Unlike many U.S. airlines, JetBlue has built a strong reputation among pet owners thanks to its **JetPaws®** program, dedicated entirely to passengers travelling with cats and dogs.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- JetBlue's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, routing and pet availability.

MyDogCanFly evaluates all three together before recommending an itinerary.

# Is JetBlue a good choice?

Yes. JetBlue is widely considered one of the most pet-friendly airlines in the United States for passengers travelling with **small dogs**. Its strongest advantages include the dedicated JetPaws® program, an excellent customer experience, numerous nonstop domestic routes, and a simple booking process for pets.

One important limitation should nevertheless be considered: JetBlue **does not transport pets in the aircraft hold**. Only small cats and dogs travelling in the cabin are accepted.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_jetblue | cabin_policy}}

JetBlue accepts only **small dogs and cats travelling in the cabin**. One pet is permitted per passenger, and the combined weight of the pet and carrier must not exceed **20 lb (9 kg)**. Pets must remain inside an approved carrier under the seat throughout the flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_jetblue | hold_policy}}

JetBlue **does not accept pets as checked baggage or in the cargo hold**, except in very limited operational circumstances unrelated to standard pet travel.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify that your dog meets the cabin size requirements;
- reserve your pet immediately;
- confirm destination-country regulations;
- verify every flight on your itinerary.

JetBlue limits the number of pets accepted on each flight, so early booking is strongly recommended.

# Connections

JetBlue performs best on nonstop domestic itineraries. If another airline operates part of your trip, verify that its pet policy is compatible with JetBlue's, particularly if your itinerary would require cargo transport.

# Common mistakes

- Assuming large dogs can travel in cargo.
- Booking before reserving the pet.
- Forgetting destination-country requirements.
- Assuming partner airlines follow JetBlue's rules.

# Expert advice

JetBlue is one of the best airlines in North America **if your dog is small enough to travel in the cabin**. If your dog is too large for cabin transport, another airline will almost certainly be a better option.
