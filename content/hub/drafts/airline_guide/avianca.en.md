# Flying with a Dog on Avianca
## Overview
Avianca is the flag carrier of Colombia and one of the oldest airlines in the world. From its primary hubs at **Bogotá El Dorado International Airport (BOG)**, **Medellín (MDE)** and **San Salvador (SAL)**, Avianca serves an extensive network throughout **South America, Central America, North America and Europe**.
Avianca accepts pets on many domestic and international routes. Depending on your dog's size, destination and operational requirements, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin** or **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**. Certain oversized animals or specific itineraries may require cargo transport.
Travelling successfully with a dog always depends on three factors:
- Avianca's pet transport policy;
- veterinary import requirements of the destination country;
- operational restrictions related to aircraft type and routing.
MyDogCanFly analyses all three before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Avianca a good choice?
Yes.
Avianca is an excellent option for travellers flying within Latin America or between the Americas and Europe. Its extensive regional network and frequent international services make it a practical choice for pet owners.
As with every international journey, always verify both the airline's transport policy and the veterinary import requirements of your destination.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify destination-country veterinary requirements;
- reserve pet transport as early as possible;
- prepare all health certificates;
- ensure your kennel complies with IATA standards;
- confirm pet acceptance on every flight segment.
---
# Common Mistakes
## Booking pet transport too late
Flights accept only a limited number of animals.
---
## Assuming every destination has identical import rules
Requirements vary considerably between countries.
---
## Using a non-compliant kennel
Only IATA-approved kennels are accepted.
---
## Forgetting transit-country regulations
Transit airports may impose additional veterinary requirements.
---
# Expert Advice
Avianca is particularly well suited for travellers flying throughout Latin America.
If your itinerary includes codeshare flights operated by another airline, verify that every carrier involved accepts pets under compatible conditions.
