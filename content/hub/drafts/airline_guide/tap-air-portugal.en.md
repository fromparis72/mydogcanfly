# Flying with a Dog on TAP Air Portugal

## Overview

TAP Air Portugal is Portugal's national airline and a member of Star Alliance. Operating primarily from Lisbon and Porto, it connects Europe with North America, South America, Africa and numerous destinations across Portugal, including Madeira and the Azores.

For travellers flying with a dog, TAP is one of Europe's most important airlines thanks to its strong transatlantic network, particularly between Europe and Brazil, as well as Portugal and North America.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- TAP's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, connections and available pet capacity.

MyDogCanFly evaluates all three together before recommending an itinerary.

# Is TAP Air Portugal a good choice?

Yes, especially if Portugal is part of your itinerary. TAP offers one of the strongest route networks between Europe and Brazil, while also serving many destinations in North America and Africa. Remaining on a single airline usually simplifies travelling with a dog by reducing policy differences between carriers.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin travel: {{fait: airline_tap_air_portugal | cabin_policy}}

Only dogs and cats may travel as pets. Cabin transport is subject to weight, carrier dimensions and space availability. Reservations must generally be requested at least 24 hours before departure.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_tap_air_portugal | hold_policy}}

Large dogs may travel in the aircraft hold using an IATA-compliant crate, subject to aircraft limitations and destination-specific restrictions. Certain brachycephalic breeds are not accepted in the hold because of increased health risks.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- confirm your dog's travel weight;
- measure the carrier or crate;
- review destination-country regulations.

TAP limits the number of pets accepted on each flight, so booking early is recommended.

# Lisbon connections

Lisbon Airport is TAP's principal hub. If your itinerary includes a connection, verify that every operating airline accepts pets under compatible conditions. Whenever possible, choose a comfortable connection rather than the shortest available transfer.

# Common mistakes

- Booking before reserving the pet.
- Assuming all Star Alliance airlines have identical pet policies.
- Forgetting destination-country veterinary requirements.
- Choosing a carrier or crate that does not comply with TAP's specifications.

# Expert advice

TAP Air Portugal is one of the strongest choices for travellers flying between Europe and Brazil. Whenever possible, keep the itinerary entirely on TAP-operated flights to minimise policy differences and simplify your journey with your dog.
