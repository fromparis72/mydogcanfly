# Flying with a Dog on WestJet

## Overview

WestJet is Canada's second-largest airline and serves an extensive network across Canada, the United States, Mexico, the Caribbean and Europe. Its hubs in Calgary, Toronto and Vancouver make it one of the most important carriers for travellers flying with pets within Canada.

For dog owners, WestJet offers several transport options, including travel in the cabin, as checked baggage and, when appropriate, through WestJet Cargo.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- WestJet's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

MyDogCanFly analyses these three layers together before recommending an itinerary.

# Is WestJet a good choice?

Yes. WestJet is particularly attractive for travellers looking for direct flights within Canada or between Canada and popular leisure destinations. Its strengths include an extensive domestic Canadian network, numerous direct routes to vacation destinations, several pet transport options, and a straightforward booking process. For simple itineraries, WestJet is often one of the easiest airlines for travelling with a dog.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_westjet | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs and cats may travel in the cabin inside an approved soft-sided carrier placed completely under the seat. Pet space must be reserved in advance because the number of animals allowed in the cabin is limited.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_westjet | hold_policy}}

Larger dogs may travel as checked animals in a temperature-controlled compartment inside an IATA-compliant crate. Some animals may instead travel through WestJet Cargo depending on the itinerary.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- check your dog's travel weight;
- measure your carrier or crate;
- review destination-country requirements.

WestJet recommends reserving your pet as soon as your booking is confirmed. If pet space has not been requested at least **48 hours before departure**, availability may no longer exist.

# Connections

Whenever possible, choose a direct flight. If your itinerary includes another airline, verify that every operating carrier accepts pets under compatible conditions. WestJet does not allow pets on certain codeshare or interline itineraries.

# Common mistakes

- Booking the ticket before reserving the pet.
- Choosing a carrier that exceeds the permitted dimensions.
- Forgetting seasonal temperature restrictions.
- Assuming pets are accepted on every codeshare itinerary.

# Expert advice

WestJet is one of Canada's best airlines for travelling with a dog, particularly on domestic routes. Whenever possible, choose a nonstop WestJet-operated flight. Fewer connections generally mean a smoother and less stressful journey for your dog.

### Should I reserve early?

Yes. WestJet limits the number of pets accepted on every flight and recommends booking your pet as early as possible, ideally more than 48 hours before departure.

### Do I still need destination-country documents?

Always. Airline approval never replaces veterinary or customs requirements imposed by the destination country.
