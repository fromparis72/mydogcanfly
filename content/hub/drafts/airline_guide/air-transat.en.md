# Flying with a Dog on Air Transat

## Overview

Air Transat is one of Canada's leading leisure airlines, connecting Montréal, Toronto and Québec City with Europe, the Caribbean, the United States, Mexico and selected destinations in South America.

For travellers flying with a dog, Air Transat is particularly attractive for holiday travel between Canada and Europe. Its seasonal network and numerous direct flights reduce the need for multiple airline changes, making journeys simpler for both passengers and pets.

A successful trip depends on three independent elements:

- Air Transat's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, seasonal temperatures, routing and available pet capacity.

MyDogCanFly evaluates all three before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Air Transat a good choice?

Yes. Air Transat is particularly well suited to leisure travellers who prefer direct seasonal routes between Canada and Europe. Its strengths include numerous non-stop transatlantic flights, a straightforward leisure-oriented network, a modern fleet, and fewer airline changes on many holiday routes. If your itinerary remains entirely on Air Transat, travelling with your dog is generally easier to organise.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_air_transat | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs and cats may travel in the cabin inside an approved carrier that fits completely under the seat. Reservations are mandatory because the number of pets accepted in the cabin is limited.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_air_transat | hold_policy}}

Larger dogs may travel in the temperature-controlled baggage compartment inside an IATA-compliant crate, subject to aircraft limitations and seasonal weather restrictions.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_air_transat | breed_restrictions}}

Always remember that airline approval does not replace destination-country veterinary requirements.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- confirm your dog's travel weight;
- measure your carrier or crate;
- review destination-country import regulations.

Pet reservations should be requested as early as possible because only a limited number of animals are accepted on each flight.

# Montréal connections

Montréal-Trudeau Airport is Air Transat's primary hub. If another airline operates part of your itinerary, verify that every carrier accepts pets under compatible conditions. Whenever possible, choose an itinerary operated entirely by Air Transat.

# Common mistakes

- Booking the ticket before reserving the pet.
- Choosing a carrier that exceeds the permitted dimensions.
- Forgetting seasonal temperature restrictions.
- Ignoring destination-country veterinary requirements.

# Expert advice

Air Transat is one of the easiest Canadian leisure airlines for travelling with a dog. Its direct transatlantic routes frequently provide a simpler and less stressful journey than itineraries requiring several airline changes.
