# Flying with a Dog on Condor

## Overview

Condor is Germany's best-known leisure airline, connecting Frankfurt with dozens of holiday destinations across Europe, North America, the Caribbean, Africa and Asia. It is especially popular with travellers flying to beach destinations or long-haul vacation resorts.

Unlike many leisure airlines, Condor accepts both small pets in the cabin and larger pets in the cargo hold, provided the airline's requirements are met. Pets must be booked in advance because capacity is limited.

# Is Condor a good choice?

Condor is an excellent choice for leisure travellers who want to fly directly from Germany to holiday destinations without changing airlines. For long-haul vacations, remaining on a single carrier usually simplifies travelling with a dog.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin: {{fait: airline_condor | cabin_policy}}

Condor accepts one cat or dog per carrier. The combined weight of the animal and carrier must not exceed **8 kg**, and the carrier must fit under the seat (maximum 55 × 40 × 23 cm). Reservations can generally be made up to 24 hours before departure, subject to availability.

Hold: {{fait: airline_condor | hold_policy}}

Pets travelling in the hold must use an IATA-compliant crate and be registered before departure. On direct flights, transport in the cargo hold is available, but it is **not available on certain connecting itineraries operated with partner airlines**.

# Before booking

Before buying your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- measure your carrier carefully;
- check destination-country requirements;
- avoid unnecessary airline changes.

# Frankfurt connections

Condor works best on direct flights. If your itinerary includes another airline before or after Condor, verify that pets can remain accepted throughout the journey, especially in the cargo hold.

# Common mistakes

- Booking before reserving the pet.
- Choosing a carrier that exceeds the permitted dimensions.
- Assuming partner-airline connections follow Condor's policy.
- Forgetting destination-country import requirements.

# Expert advice

Condor is one of the easiest European leisure airlines for travelling with a dog, but it performs best on **non-stop itineraries**. If your trip requires multiple airlines, compare alternatives before booking.
