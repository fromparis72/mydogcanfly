# Flying with a Dog on Smartwings

## Overview

Smartwings is the largest airline in the Czech Republic and operates scheduled and leisure flights throughout Europe, North Africa and the Middle East. Following the integration of Czech Airlines (CSA), Smartwings now operates many routes previously flown under the CSA brand.

For travellers flying with a dog, Smartwings is primarily a leisure airline. It accepts dogs and cats on many routes, but availability is limited and some destinations have specific restrictions.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Smartwings' pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather and available pet capacity.

# Is Smartwings a good choice?

Smartwings is a practical option for direct leisure flights. Its strongest advantage is avoiding unnecessary airline changes on holiday routes. If your itinerary requires multiple airlines or long-haul connections, comparing alternatives is recommended.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- confirm your dog's travel weight;
- measure your carrier or crate;
- review destination-country requirements.

Pet transport must normally be booked **at least 48 hours before departure**.

# Prague connections

Smartwings performs best on direct leisure routes. If another airline operates part of your itinerary, verify that its pet policy is compatible with Smartwings.

# Common mistakes

- Booking before reserving the pet.
- Using a carrier larger than permitted.
- Forgetting route-specific restrictions.
- Ignoring destination-country import requirements.

# Expert advice

Smartwings is best suited to simple point-to-point leisure travel. For complex international itineraries involving connections, a network carrier may provide a smoother experience for travellers with dogs.
