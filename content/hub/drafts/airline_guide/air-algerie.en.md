# Flying with a Dog on Air Algérie
## Overview
Air Algérie is the national airline of Algeria. From its primary hub at **Houari Boumediene Airport in Algiers (ALG)**, the airline serves an extensive network across **Europe, North Africa, Sub-Saharan Africa, the Middle East and selected destinations in North America**.
Air Algérie accepts companion animals on many domestic and international flights. Depending on your dog's size, the route and operational constraints, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin**, **in the aircraft's climate-controlled hold**, or **as cargo** when required.
Successful pet travel always depends on three elements:
- Air Algérie's transport policy;
- veterinary import requirements of the destination country;
- operational restrictions related to the aircraft and itinerary.
MyDogCanFly analyses all three before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Air Algérie a good choice?
Yes.
Air Algérie is a practical choice for travellers flying between Algeria and Europe, particularly France, Spain and Italy. Its dense regional network also makes it useful for journeys across North Africa and the Sahel.
As with every international airline, airline approval and destination-country veterinary requirements must both be satisfied before departure.

# Before Booking
Before purchasing your ticket:
- verify destination-country veterinary requirements;
- reserve your dog's transport as early as possible;
- prepare all required health documentation;
- ensure your kennel complies with IATA standards;
- confirm pet acceptance on every flight segment.
---
# Common Mistakes
## Booking pet transport too late
Each flight accepts only a limited number of pets.
---
## Assuming all destinations apply identical veterinary rules
Import requirements differ from country to country.
---
## Using a kennel that does not comply with IATA regulations
Only approved kennels are accepted.
---
## Forgetting transit-country requirements
Transit airports may have additional animal transport regulations.
---
# Expert Advice
Air Algérie is particularly useful for travellers flying between Europe and Algeria.
If your itinerary includes onward connections, verify pet acceptance on every flight before purchasing non-refundable tickets.
