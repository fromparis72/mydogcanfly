# Flying with a Dog on Air Canada

## Overview

Air Canada is Canada's flag carrier and a founding member of **Star Alliance**. From its major hubs at **Toronto (YYZ), Montréal (YUL), Vancouver (YVR)** and **Calgary (YYC)**, the airline serves more than 180 destinations across North America, Europe, Asia, South America and Oceania.

For travellers flying with a dog, Air Canada is one of North America's most flexible airlines. Depending on your dog's size, breed and destination, pets may travel **in the passenger cabin**, **in the aircraft's climate-controlled baggage compartment**, or via **Air Canada Cargo (AC Animals)** for oversized animals or shipments exceeding standard limits.

A successful journey depends on three independent elements:

- Air Canada's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and available pet capacity.

# Is Air Canada a good choice?

Yes. Air Canada is one of the most pet-friendly major airlines in North America. Its strengths include pets accepted both in the cabin and in the baggage compartment, an extensive domestic and international network, a dedicated **AC Animals** cargo service, a modern long-haul fleet, and clear online guidance for pet travel. For owners of both small and large dogs, Air Canada offers considerably more flexibility than many U.S. carriers.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin transport: {{fait: airline_air_canada | cabin_policy}}

Small dogs may travel in the passenger cabin inside a **soft-sided carrier** that fits completely beneath the seat in front of you. Beginning **1 June 2025**, Air Canada requires all in-cabin pets to travel in soft-sided carriers. Cabin pets are not permitted in certain seating locations (such as exit rows or bulkhead seats), and space is limited on every flight.

Hold transport: {{fait: airline_air_canada | hold_policy}}

Dogs exceeding cabin limits may travel in the aircraft's ventilated and temperature-controlled baggage compartment inside an **IATA-compliant kennel**. On most routes, the combined weight of the dog and kennel must not exceed **45 kg (100 lb)**. Larger kennels or heavier animals generally require transport through **Air Canada Cargo (AC Animals)**. Seasonal heat restrictions also apply on certain routes and destinations.

Breed restrictions: {{fait: airline_air_canada | breed_restrictions}}

Certain brachycephalic breeds and specific operational situations may result in additional restrictions. Heat embargoes may also temporarily prevent transport in the baggage compartment.

# Before booking

Before confirming your reservation:

- verify destination-country import requirements;
- ensure your dog is properly identified and vaccinated;
- choose an IATA-compliant carrier or kennel;
- register your pet with Air Canada.

Air Canada strongly recommends registering pets **within 24 hours of booking** to guarantee availability. Pets that have not been registered in advance may not be accepted for travel.

# Connecting through Canada

Toronto, Montréal and Vancouver are major international hubs. Whenever possible, remain on Air Canada-operated flights, verify every codeshare segment, and avoid long connections if your dog travels in the baggage compartment. Remember that many partner airlines apply different pet policies.

# Common mistakes

## Waiting too long before registering your dog

Pet capacity is limited on every aircraft.

## Using a hard-sided carrier in the cabin

Since June 2025, Air Canada requires **soft-sided carriers** for all pets travelling in the cabin.

## Forgetting seasonal embargoes

Some destinations prohibit baggage-compartment pet transport during hot weather.

## Booking codeshare flights without checking

Not every flight marketed by Air Canada is operated by Air Canada, and partner airlines may refuse pets.

# Expert advice

Air Canada is one of the strongest choices in North America for travelling with a dog. Unlike several U.S. airlines, it still offers both **cabin** and **checked hold** transport on many routes, while also providing a dedicated cargo solution for oversized animals. For the smoothest experience, register your dog immediately after booking your ticket and keep your itinerary entirely on Air Canada-operated flights whenever possible.

Yes. Air Canada recommends registering your pet within **24 hours of booking**, as cabin and hold capacity is limited.
