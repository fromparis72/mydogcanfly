# Flying with a Dog on Finnair

## Overview

Finnair is Finland's national airline and a member of the oneworld alliance. Operating primarily from Helsinki Airport, it connects Northern Europe with Asia, North America and numerous European destinations.

For travellers flying with a dog, Finnair is well known for its efficient Helsinki hub and straightforward pet policies. It is particularly attractive for journeys between Europe and Northern Europe, as well as many intercontinental routes.

A successful trip always depends on three independent elements:

- Finnair's pet transport policy;
- destination-country veterinary and customs requirements;
- operational constraints such as aircraft type, weather, routing and pet availability.

MyDogCanFly evaluates these three elements together before recommending an itinerary.

# Is Finnair a good choice?

Yes. Finnair is recognised for its punctuality, efficient connections through Helsinki and relatively simple transfer experience. It is particularly attractive for travellers looking for a premium airline without the complexity of very large European hubs.

# Cabin, hold or cargo

Cabin: {{fait: airline_finnair | cabin_policy}}

Small cats and dogs may travel in the cabin when the combined weight of the pet and carrier does not exceed **8 kg** and the carrier fits under the seat.

Hold: {{fait: airline_finnair | hold_policy}}

Larger dogs may travel in the temperature-controlled hold. On Finnair-operated flights, the combined weight of the pet and crate may be up to **75 kg**.

# Before booking

Before purchasing your ticket:

- verify pet availability;
- check your dog's travel weight;
- measure your carrier or crate;
- review destination-country requirements.

Finnair recommends booking pet transport well in advance because the number of animals accepted on each flight is limited.

# Helsinki connections

Helsinki Airport is one of Europe's most efficient transfer hubs. If your itinerary includes a connection, ensure every operating airline accepts pets under compatible conditions. For pets travelling in the hold, Finnair recommends allowing at least **3 hours** for connections.

# Common mistakes

- Booking flights before confirming pet availability.
- Choosing a carrier that exceeds the permitted dimensions.
- Ignoring destination-country import requirements.
- Assuming partner airlines follow identical pet policies.

# Expert advice

Finnair is one of the strongest choices for travel between Europe and Asia thanks to its short northern routes and efficient hub. Whenever possible, keep your itinerary entirely on Finnair-operated flights to simplify your journey with your dog.
