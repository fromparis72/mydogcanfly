This involves adhering to and enforcing international and national standards for animals and animal products, preventing health problems in humans and animals and environmental pollution caused by related products and technologies, and increasing market acceptance and competitiveness. Expected Outcomes Increased supply of animal inputs with verified quality and safety. Animal product inputs with guaranteed quality and safety. Reduced antimicrobial resistance (AMR) in germs/pathogens. Strengthened control of animal disease risks. Live animals and animal products with certified health, quality, and safety that are acceptable in both domestic and international markets.
