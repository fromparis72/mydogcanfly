TRAVEL WITH PETS: canines or felines ENTRY TO URUGUAY

In order to introduce your pet (cat or dog) to Uruguay, it is mandatory to obtain an International Health Certificate issued and signed by the Competent Authorities from the country of origin. 

This certificate should be issued within 10 days prior to the trip.

 

Model form: International Veterinary Certificate

Model form: Zoosanitary Requirements - Domestic Dogs and Cats

Model form: Regulation of Leishmania
