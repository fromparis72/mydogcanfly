Jurisdictions

 

Lebanon has one Embassy and three Consulates General in the United States, each consulate covers a specific jurisdiction.
Honorary Consulates serve the residents of: Raleigh (NC), Birmingham (AL), Cleveland (OH), Phoenix (AZ), Las Vegas (NV), San Francisco (CA), Atlanta (GA), and Tampa (FL)
