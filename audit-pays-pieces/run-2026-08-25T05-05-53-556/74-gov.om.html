Get a Veterinary Permit to Import Animals Shipment

This service enables you to get a veterinary permit to import an animal shipment.

Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries Wealth & Water Resources
Updated: July 02, 2026
You are starting the service as:
Personal account
gup-business-account-label

Required documents

  • 1. Commercial Register
  • 2. Civil ID or Resident Card
  • 3. Farm or Facility License
  • 4. Health Documents (Depending on the Type of Permit) - Some health certificates are accompanied by the inspection service within a period not exceeding 48 hours: • Vaccinations • Blood tests • Government veterinary health certificate • Quarantine certificate for live animals • Halal slaughter certificates • Accreditation of veterinary quarantines in the exporting country • Accreditation of slaughterhouses • Accreditation of pharmaceutical companies • Facility license (local) • Accreditation of factories • Accreditation of farms • Drug analysis certificates • Manufacturing quality certificates • Vaccination passports • Certificate of no objection from the Environment Authority (local) • Certificate of freedom of circulation • Certificate of origin

Service steps

  1. 1

    The beneficiary submits the application through Bayan system

  2. 2

    Review of the application

  3. 3

    Approval

  4. 4

    Payment of fees

  5. 5

    Issuance of the permit electronically

Special conditions

  • 2. The commodity, its type, classification, quantity, origin, and weight must be determined
  • 3. If it is an individual, the civil card (citizen) or resident card is required
  • 4. Vehicle ownership (owned by the individual or the company)
  • 5. A positive result for the risk analysis
  • 6. It meets the health requirements for importing accompanying goods (licensed farm or licensed facility to deal with these types of animals, licensed establishments (depending on the type of permit): • Pet stores • Live birds • Circus show • Zoo • Aquarium
  • 7. Completion of health documents (all from outside the Sultanate) which are (based on the type of permit): • Vaccines • Blood tests • Government veterinary health certificate • Quarantine certificate for living animals • Halal certificates • Approving veterinary quarantine in the exporting country • Approving slaughterhouses • Approving pharmaceutical companies • Approving factories • Facility license (local) • Approving farms • Medicines analysis certificates • Manufacturing quality certificates • vaccine passport • No objection letter from the Environment Authority (local) • Trading freedom certificate • Certificate of Origin
  • 1. If it is a company, a commercial registration with a commercial activity to import accompanying goods is required
