MyDogCanFly — brand assets
==========================

WHAT IS IN THIS PACK

  mydogcanfly-emblem.*      The flying dog, on its own.
  mydogcanfly-logo.*        The full lockup: emblem and wordmark side by side.
  mydogcanfly-wordmark.*    "MyDogCanFly.com" set as a logotype.

  Each comes in three colour variants:
    (no suffix)   full colour — navy #011D48 with the orange accent #F2700F
    -mono         single-colour navy, for one-ink printing
    -white        single-colour cream #FCFBF9, for dark backgrounds

  And in three formats:
    .svg          true vector — scales to any size without loss
    .pdf          true vector — for print workflows
    .png          transparent background, 2400 px (emblem) / 3000 px (wordmark)

CLEAR SPACE

  Keep free space around the logo equal to the height of the "M" in MyDogCanFly.
  Minimum width: 24 mm in print, 120 px on screen for the wordmark.

WHAT NOT TO DO

  Do not stretch, rotate, recolour or add effects.
  Do not place the colour version on a dark or busy background — use -white.
COLOURS

  Ink        #011D48
  Deep       #0E2350
  Accent     #F2700F
  Paper      #FCFBF9
